A showcase in Waseca brought Italian sports cars and prize-winning horse bloodlines together on the same lawn. Longtime equestrian breeders Walter and Cheryl Mishek opened their 75-acre property, Calonett, for an event titled "Ferrari x Calonett: Two Kinds of Horsepower" on Saturday, Oct. 10, 2026, Southernminn.com reported. The estate at 14720 Old U.S. Highway 14 is listed for $5.5 million through Michelle Kalina, a global real estate advisor at Lakes Sotheby’s International Realty.

The $5.5 million asking price makes Calonett the highest-priced residential listing in the local market. Kalina told Southernminn.com that the median home price in the Waseca area is roughly $250,000, while the previous local record sale was $625,000. She added that the property ranks as the fourth-highest price per acre currently listed in Minnesota.

The weekend showcase featured a parade and lineup of privately owned Ferraris, including a 488 GTB, 599 GTB Fiorano, 458 Italia, F8 Spider, and Purosangue, alongside riding tours led by Daniel of Chavez Horsemanship. Two attendees registered preliminary buying interest during the gathering. Kalina stated that the ideal buyer will likely be a legacy-minded purchaser looking for a multigenerational family compound or private retreat.

Walter Mishek's father originally acquired the land in 1969. Mishek founded The Arabian Horse Times above his family's garage in Waseca in 1970, turning the publication into an international journal of record while building Calonett into a breeding operation, according to Southernminn.com and The Business Journals. Operating as Mishek Arabians, the farm produced 1983 U.S. National Champion Mare Bask Calonett and 1996 World Champion Stallion Focus Khemali. Mishek explained to Southernminn.com that he always loved Arabian halter horses, which compete in show ring beauty contests, and wanted them as his livelihood.

The primary residence spans 11,009 finished square feet. It was rebuilt in 2004, with additions completed in 2020 and 2023. The layout includes six bedrooms, nine bathrooms, two executive offices, a sunroom, a home gym, a movie theater, a passenger elevator, and four separate laundry rooms.

Inside, the home features custom Brazilian onyx countertops, handcrafted cherry millwork, and an owner's suite with dual dressing rooms, according to The Business Journals and Lakes Sotheby’s International Realty. The floor plan incorporates a walk-in temperature-controlled storage room for wine and collectibles, a private lounge with mirror-finished walls and ceilings, a commercial-style catering kitchen, an entertaining ballroom, a six-car garage, zoned in-floor radiant heating, and a whole-house backup generator.

Outside the house, the 75 acres hold fenced pastures, paddocks, and a six-acre stocked pond with a fountain. The grounds also feature a heated multipurpose horse barn and pavilion with 14-foot vaulted ceilings, along with finished living quarters for caretakers or guests.

Mishek explained that the couple decided to sell because maintaining Calonett alongside residences in Saratoga, Wyoming, and Palm Springs, California, became unsustainable. He told Southernminn.com that while they cannot handle three homes, there is not one thing he does not love about the Waseca estate.
