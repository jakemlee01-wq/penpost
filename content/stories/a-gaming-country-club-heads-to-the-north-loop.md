Management consultants Adam and Bonnie Baker bought the historic two-story commercial building at 119 Washington Ave. N. for $1.83 million to open a private gaming parlor, according to reporting by the Minneapolis / St. Paul Business Journal.

The Bakers purchased the 7,200-square-foot property under the entity 119 Washington Holding LLC. The 1913 structure was sold by the family of the late restaurateur John Rimarcik, who was an early booster of the North Loop neighborhood. Craft cocktail bar Public Domain has operated on the ground floor since late 2023 and will remain open there.

Coldwell Banker Realty brokers Craig Weber and Shane Spencer represented the Bakers and also invested in the property. Colliers brokers John McCarthy, Terese Reiling, and Tom Sullivan represented the Rimarcik family. The deal marked the Bakers' first commercial real estate purchase and followed an approximate one-year property search.

On the second floor, the Bakers are launching the Gamer's Guild Social Club, scheduled to open in November 2026. The North Loop Neighborhood Association reported that the concept is billed as a country club for gamers, with tables for board games, tabletop role-playing games, classic cards, mahjong, and miniatures. The club website states an ambition to create the preeminent gaming-focused social club in the world.

Adam Baker grew up playing card and board games with family, and his 27-year-old son Christopher reintroduced him to Magic: The Gathering. That spark led to the gaming country club idea in September 2025. Baker told the Minneapolis / St. Paul Business Journal that he hopes the space will serve as an antidote to post-pandemic isolation and workplace fatigue, adding that everybody is burned out with work.

The venture is starting with 20 founding memberships, mostly drawn from friends and family. Those founding members commit to a two-year buy-in and receive an equity stake, which is an ownership share in the business. Sixteen of those 20 founding spots have been claimed.

Broader membership recruitment is planned for early 2027. Monthly dues reported by the North Loop Neighborhood Association start at $250 for an individual with guest privileges. Couples memberships range from $417 to $833 per month and also allow guests.
