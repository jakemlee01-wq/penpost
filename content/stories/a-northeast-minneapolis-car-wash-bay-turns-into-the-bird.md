A former standalone car wash bay in an active BP gas station parking lot in Northeast Minneapolis has reopened as The Bird, a walk-up fried chicken eatery. The site is located at 2638 University Avenue NE, at the corner of University and 27th avenues northeast, according to reporting by Bring Me The News.

A full interior and exterior renovation transformed the small footprint into a working commercial kitchen. The business operates strictly through an exterior ordering and pickup window. Customers can place orders in person, online, or by calling ahead, with larger group orders packaged as "Flock Deals" meant to feed four to five people.

The Bird brands its menu as made-to-order "funk-a-fried" chicken that is "crispy with character" and designed with an "unreasonable personality." Chicken selections include fresh-to-order fried wings, tenders known as "tendies," bite-sized chicken, sandwiches, and wraps called "The Wraptor."

Bring Me The News reported that the founders drew culinary inspiration from their Lebanese roots. A spicy house Lebanese sauce serves as the signature item, offered alongside a lineup of eight dipping sauces that include "Hot Honey Buffalo" and a high-heat recipe called "Atomic Cluck."

The menu also features comfort sides, including baked mac and cheese, fried pickles, and loaded french fries. For dessert and drinks, the kitchen serves fruit smoothies, slices of cheesecake, banana pudding, a dessert called "Cluckin' Good Cake," and milkshakes blended in flavors such as Oreo, cookie dough, and strawberry banana.

The eatery operates seven days a week, opening daily at 11:00 a.m. Closing time is 10:00 p.m. Sunday through Thursday, with service extended until 11:00 p.m. on Friday and Saturday nights.

Management stated that as the brand establishes its footing in Northeast Minneapolis, it plans to expand the menu with seasonal flavors and introduce new community offerings across the city.
