The Northfield Arts Guild opens the Midwest premiere of Caddyshakes on Friday, October 23, 2026. The stage comedy adapts the 1980 golf movie Caddyshack into the style and language of William Shakespeare.

Local playwright Michael Schaeffer wrote and directed the production, according to the Northfield Arts Guild. Schaeffer first developed the script while serving on the board of the Fairbanks Shakespeare Theatre in Alaska. KYMN Radio reported that the Alaska company needed a fundraiser after previously mounting Shakespearean adaptations of The Big Lebowski and Star Wars. During an appearance on KYMN's Art Zany program, Schaeffer stated that he wanted to work with a movie he loved and would not mind watching in 30-second increments.

The resulting script weaves in lines, insults, rhyming couplets, and plot twists drawn from each of Shakespeare's 37 plays, KYMN reported. Bill Murray's greenskeeper character Carl Spackler transforms into Carliolanus, who borrows lines from the Macbeth dagger soliloquy during his poolside scene. Other renamed figures on the fairway include Praetor Prospero Smails and Hal Czervik.

Schaeffer also worked local geography into the script. KYMN reported that the dialogue includes comedic references to Carleton College, St. Olaf College, Owatonna, and the Dundas Yacht Club.

Thirteen performers make up the cast, including nine local actors and four from Savage, Rosemount, and the Twin Cities, according to KYMN. Andy Anderson plays the brash golf-course developer role originated on film by Rodney Dangerfield. Anderson told KYMN that he made a deliberate choice not to deliver a simple Dangerfield impersonation.

Joe Brennan, who works at the Northfield bakery Bread People, makes his stage debut in the show. KYMN reported that Brennan operates the puppet for the story's gopher while also taking on multiple acting roles, including the pool lifeguard and Ariel from The Tempest.

A rotating role also brings someone from the crowd into the cast each evening. The Arts Guild and KYMN reported that a different audience volunteer or pre-show auction bidder plays the apothecary, remaining on stage across several scenes.

Performances take place at the Northfield Arts Guild Theater at 411 West 3rd Street in Northfield. General admission tickets cost $22, while tickets for seniors and students cost $18, according to the Guild.

The production runs through November 1, 2026. Evening performances start at 7:30 p.m. on Friday, October 23, Friday, October 30, and Saturday, October 31. Matinee shows begin at 2:00 p.m. on Saturday, October 24, Sunday, October 25, and Sunday, November 1.

The Guild scheduled a question-and-answer session with Schaeffer and the cast immediately following the October 25 matinee. The Halloween night show on October 31 features an optional costume contest with prizes awarded at intermission.
