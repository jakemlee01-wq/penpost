Jada Ewing, the owner of the home-based cottage baking business Southern Mama Goodies in Albany, has started a campaign to buy a storefront in town, one portion of cake at a time.

According to reporting by WJON, Ewing launched the "20,000 slice challenge" on September 18, 2026. Her goal is to raise $200,000 to purchase an existing brick-and-mortar bakery in Albany that recently went up for sale.

To finance the acquisition, Ewing bakes 10-inch cakes that yield 14 slices each, selling individual slices for $10 each. Customers can also buy an entire cake for $140. Every day at 5:00 p.m., Ewing hosts a flavor drop on her business page featuring a newly baked flavor. The 14 slices are available on a first-come, first-served basis. Buyers reserve their portions online and receive a private message to coordinate payment and local pickup.

Ewing reported to WJON that she had sold 136 physical slices by the second week of October. She described the effort as a random idea that blew up in the best way possible.

For supporters who live outside Central Minnesota, Ewing created a virtual slice option online. Once 14 virtual slices are purchased, accounting for one full cake, Ewing bakes and hand-delivers that cake to local teachers, schools, nurses, police stations, or area small businesses.

Ewing is a self-taught baker who first took up baking as a personal hobby in 2020. She previously worked full-time as a hospice certified nursing assistant, but she recently resigned from that position to focus entirely on baking and completing the challenge.
