Brooklyn Marie Ostlund, a 14-year-old freshman at Alexandria Area High School who performs as Brooklyn Marie, has advanced to the Battle Rounds on Season 30 of NBC's "The Voice."

Both KARE 11 and WJON reported that Ostlund earned her spot by performing an acoustic rendition of Olivia Rodrigo's ballad "traitor" while playing guitar during the blind auditions. Coaches Queen Latifah and Kelly Clarkson both turned their chairs for her performance, according to WJON. Queen Latifah pressed her button almost immediately, while Clarkson turned near the end of the song.

According to WJON, Queen Latifah exclaimed "What is this?!" after learning Ostlund was 14. Queen Latifah told her, "Your voice sounded so special to me right away that I was like, 'What's happening here?' And then I turn around, and you just sing that song like that as you play guitar." Returning coach Adam Levine, who is currently tied with Clarkson at four career wins, stated on air that he regretted not hitting his button.

Ostlund chose to join Team Kelly. As KARE 11 reported, Ostlund cited Clarkson's history of mentoring teenage competitors, including past 15-year-old champion Brynn Cartelli. Clarkson made her pitch by noting her own background, telling Ostlund, "I knew you were young; I did not think 14. I do love working a lot with the younger talent because there's a freedom about your spirits, and whenever you're picking songs, and you're very bold. I've also navigated a competition myself as a teenager and I know how to do it, girl, and I know how to win this show," according to WJON.

To keep her audition secret from friends and classmates, Ostlund told them she was traveling to "band camp" when she flew to Los Angeles for filming, WJON reported.

Ostlund stated that she has performed publicly since age 3, when she sang in a regional community telethon, according to WJON. Both outlets reported that she took up guitar at age 7 and also plays piano, violin, and percussion. Since age 11, she has played two- to four-hour paid gigs at wineries, breweries, weddings, and community festivals across the Alexandria Lakes Area, WJON reported.

WJON reported that Ostlund is a straight-A student who deposits her gig earnings into a savings fund for future medical school tuition, aiming to balance a medical career with her music.

Ostlund is scheduled to sing the National Anthem at U.S. Bank Stadium before the Minnesota Vikings game on Sunday, October 25, 2026, according to WJON. Her Battle Round matchup against a fellow member of Team Kelly is scheduled to broadcast on NBC the next evening, Monday, October 26, 2026, at 7 p.m. Central. If she advances, WJON reported that Ostlund must next clear "Cut Week" on November 2, 2026, which narrows the competition to a top 13 ahead of the live voting rounds.
