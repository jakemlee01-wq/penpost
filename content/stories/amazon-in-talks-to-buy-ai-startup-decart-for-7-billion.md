Amazon is in active discussions to acquire artificial intelligence startup Decart in a transaction that could value the company at approximately $7 billion, according to reports from The Wall Street Journal and GeekWire.

Decart builds software, including the Decart Optimization Stack, to run artificial intelligence models across multiple chip architectures, GeekWire reported. The tools are designed to help Amazon migrate developers onto its own Trainium chips.

Israeli engineers and brothers Dean and Orian Leitersdorf founded Decart in 2023 alongside Moshe Shalev, according to TradingView. The company had raised upward of $400 million in venture funding, The Wall Street Journal and TradingView reported, including a $300 million investment round in May 2026 led by Radical Ventures with Nvidia participating.

Prior to Amazon's talks, artificial intelligence rival Anthropic had explored an acquisition of Decart valued at roughly $6 billion before those negotiations halted, TradingView reported.
