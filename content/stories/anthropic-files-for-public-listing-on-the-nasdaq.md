Anthropic filed its Form S-1 registration statement with the Securities and Exchange Commission on Monday, October 26, 2026, Forbes reported. The artificial intelligence developer plans to list its Class A common stock on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker symbol "ANT," according to The VC Corner. Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are leading a 22-bank syndicate underwriting the deal, Quartz and Unite.AI reported.

The initial filing left share counts, pricing ranges, and target raise amounts blank. Market reports from The VC Corner, Seeking Alpha, and Kiplinger indicate Anthropic could seek a valuation between $1.5 trillion and $2 trillion. Those outlets note the company may attempt to raise up to $100 billion, which would surpass SpaceX's June offering as the largest initial public offering in history. The news lifted shares of semiconductor and tech firms on Monday, including Nvidia, Broadcom, and Micron, according to Alain Guillot and FinancialJuice.

The prospectus showed rapid growth alongside massive accounting charges. Anthropic generated $16.29 billion in revenue for the first six months of 2026, surging nearly 14-fold from $1.16 billion in the same period of 2025, The VC Corner and VentureBeat reported. Second-quarter revenue alone reached $11.56 billion, yielding an operating profit of $974 million for the quarter, Forbes and Tom Tunguz noted. For the full six months, the company posted an operating loss of $693 million and adjusted operating income of $1.62 billion, VentureBeat and Quartz reported. Anthropic also recorded an $80.7 billion net loss for the half, driven largely by $72.4 billion in non-cash accounting adjustments to the fair value of convertible instruments, which are debt or preferred shares that adjust in paper value as company valuations rise.

Quartz and Engadget reported that Anthropic held $112.4 billion in cash and cash equivalents, along with $19.3 billion in short-term investments, after raising $141.1 billion in private capital through June 30, 2026. Compute and infrastructure expenses reached $8.95 billion for the first six months of 2026, VentureBeat and Stock Titan reported. Anthropic outlined binding agreements with Amazon and Google totaling at least $224.5 billion through 2033 and 2036, while Tom Tunguz and Forbes reported total infrastructure commitments exceeding $500 billion across partners over the next decade.

Siblings Dario and Daniela Amodei founded the San Francisco company in 2021 as a public-benefit corporation, a corporate structure required to balance social objectives with financial returns, Tom Tunguz and Kiplinger reported. Dario Amodei received nearly $18 million in 2025 compensation, including millions for private security, according to filings cited by Business Insider. Anthropic will operate as a controlled company, with a single Class F share granting 50.1% voting power to a founder entity, Quartz and explainx.ai reported, while an independent Long-Term Benefit Trust holds the power to elect four of the seven board directors.

Anthropic reported having more than 11,900 enterprise customers, but five customers accounted for 25% of total revenue, Seeking Alpha and Quartz noted. Out of 335 pages, the prospectus devoted 94 pages to risks, detailing autonomous AI systems, customer concentration, and an active Federal Trade Commission civil investigative demand. Following a confidential filing in June, SEC rules require a 15-day waiting period before an investor roadshow begins, which Quartz and Kiplinger project for the week of November 9 ahead of an expected trading debut before Thanksgiving.
