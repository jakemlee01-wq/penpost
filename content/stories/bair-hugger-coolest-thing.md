Minnesota's coolest thing turns out to be something that keeps people warm.

Solventum's Bair Hugger Warming System won the 2026 Coolest Thing Made in Minnesota contest. The Minnesota Chamber of Commerce and BMO announced the result Tuesday at the chamber's Manufacturers' Summit in St. Paul. It beat 63 other products after five rounds of public voting.

The Bair Hugger helps keep a patient's body at a normal temperature during surgery. The company says that lowers the risk of complications from unintended hypothermia.

Solventum, which spun off from 3M in 2024, was the youngest company among the four finalists. The others were Vistabule's teardrop trailers from Eagan, ATZ Manufacturing's TrackChair all-terrain wheelchair from Marshall, and Wipaire's Fire Boss, an aerial firefighting system that scoops up water and drops it on wildfires.

The contest, now in its third year, is open only to products manufactured in Minnesota by companies that have existed for at least two years. A product doesn't have to have been invented here.

It's a popularity vote, not an engineering award, so the result says more about what caught people's attention than about which product is best. It's still a useful look at how much is made in this state, from operating rooms to campgrounds.
