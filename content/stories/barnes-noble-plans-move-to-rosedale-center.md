Long before it served waffles and tacos, the second-floor food hall space at Rosedale Center operated as a Borders bookstore between 2006 and 2011. Now, books appear ready to claim the space once again.

Rosedale Center announced that its Potluck Food Hall will shutter in mid-October after roughly seven years of operation, according to reporting from the Minneapolis / St. Paul Business Journal, FOX 9, and Bring Me The News. A source familiar with the plans told the Business Journal that Barnes & Noble intends to relocate from its longtime location at HarMar Mall into the vacated food hall.

Paperwork filed with Roseville officials points to the change. Champlin-based contractor Egan Co. submitted a building permit application titled "Barnes & Noble" on September 3, 2026. The application requests core and shell demolition inside the space alongside the installation of rooftop ventilation units, though Roseville's permit dashboard shows the permit has not yet been issued.

The Potluck space spans roughly 32,000 square feet. That makes it slightly larger than the typical 26,000-square-foot Barnes & Noble store footprint, but smaller than the retailer's current 44,000-square-foot store at HarMar Mall. Barnes & Noble has deep roots in the neighborhood, having opened its first superstore outside New York City as a 14,000-square-foot shop in a strip mall adjacent to Rosedale.

Barnes & Noble has not publicly confirmed the move. A company spokesperson stated that the store continues to operate business as usual and does not have changes to announce regarding its HarMar Mall location at this time. Rosedale Center management indicated that an official public announcement regarding the replacement tenant could arrive in early 2027.

The prospective move comes as HarMar Mall faces potential changes. Mall owner Fidelis has been evaluating redevelopment concepts that could include tearing down and rebuilding the center to add residential row houses and new retail while retaining only Cub Foods, creating uncertainty for current tenants, according to reporting by the Business Journal and Axios.

Inside Rosedale, the closing food hall will see six vendors depart: Smack Shack, OG ZaZa Pizza, Black Coffee & Waffle Bar, Legendary Spice, Boba X, and Tio's Tacos. Tenant Tio's Tacos announced its final day in the space is October 18, 2026, while original vendor Burger Dive relocated to a standalone mall space earlier in the year.
