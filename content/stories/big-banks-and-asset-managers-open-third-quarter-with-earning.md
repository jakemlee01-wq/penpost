Edward Jones reported that 85% of companies beat earnings expectations in the initial wave of S&P 500 reports, with an average surprise of 3.3%. Edward Jones and UBS projected earnings will expand 25% to 27% year over year, led by energy, technology, and communications. Combined third-quarter profits across the six largest Wall Street banks reached $43.5 billion on $165 billion in revenue, according to Global Brands Magazine and Fitch via Caijing.

BlackRock shares rose 0.7% after third-quarter profit jumped 38%, beating estimates, ATB Financial reported. Net client inflows reached $205 billion, lifting total assets under management to a record $15.49 trillion to $15.5 trillion, up from $13.46 trillion a year earlier, according to Tekedia and Pluang. Pluang reported exchange-traded funds, which pool investments and trade like individual stocks, accounted for $135 billion of those inflows.

BlackRock's adjusted diluted earnings per share reached $15.93, above consensus of $14.32 to $14.33, according to Pluang, Tekedia, and INDmoney. Net quarterly revenue climbed 19% to between $7.75 billion and $7.8 billion, operating margin reached 47.8%, and adjusted operating income rose 27%, Pluang, TradingKey, and Seeking Alpha reported. TradingKey and BNN Bloomberg noted BlackRock maintained a $5.73 quarterly dividend while integrating Global Infrastructure Partners, Preqin, and HPS Investment Partners toward a 2030 target of $35 billion in annual revenue.

CEO Larry Fink said establishing rating-agency metrics for securitized compute will take time, and BlackRock is participating in discussions on artificial intelligence and energy infrastructure, Investing.com and 24/7 Wall St. reported. CFO Martin Small stated the firm can deliver 6% to 8% organic base-fee growth, potentially reaching double digits during peak activity, according to 24/7 Wall St.

Goldman Sachs beat earnings estimates behind a 32% revenue surge in global banking and markets, ATB Financial reported. Net revenue rose 23% to $18.68 billion, net profit rose 29% to $5.29 billion, and earnings per share reached $17.01, beating consensus of $12.86 to $13.02 by about 31%, according to MarketBeat, Stocktwits, and Aardvark Labs Capital Research. Global Brands Magazine and Stocktwits reported equities trading revenue jumped 72% and investment banking fees rose 21%. CEO David Solomon likened financial tokenization to electronic trading 50 years ago, saying it creates new products, activity, clients, and distribution, Traders Union and Altcoin Buzz reported.

Morgan Stanley posted record wealth management inflows, ATB Financial reported, with Global Brands Magazine citing $232.4 billion in net new assets and a 41% climb in equities trading. INDmoney and Tiger Brokers reported net revenue of $20.3 billion and net income of $4.9 billion, yielding earnings per share of $3.03, above forecasts of $2.91 to $2.93.

Citigroup had its strongest third-quarter revenue in a decade, ATB Financial reported. Gotrade and Stocktwits reported revenue rose 8% to $23.85 billion, and net income rose 40% to $5.25 billion, or $2.85 per share, clearing consensus of $2.65. Return on tangible common equity reached 11.5%, Global Brands Magazine and Stocktwits reported. Seeking Alpha reported Citigroup raised its 2026 forecast for net interest income, which measures lending profit after deposit costs, to mid-to-high 6% growth over its 2025 benchmark of $49.8 billion.

Wells Fargo saw a 12% revenue gain in wealth and investment management, ATB Financial reported. Total net revenue rose 2% to $21.87 billion, while net income reached $5.95 billion to $6.0 billion, or $1.87 per share, clearing estimates of $1.85, according to Global Brands Magazine, Stocktwits, and Family Wealth Report. CEO Charlie Scharf stated results reflect broad-based growth, Family Wealth Report reported. Within corporate and investment banking, merger revenue rose 30%, equity capital markets fees rose 33%, and commercial banking client fees rose 25%, Family Wealth Report noted. Family Wealth Report and Seeking Alpha added Wells Fargo repurchased $2.5 billion in stock, reached 13.8% return on equity, and guided fourth-quarter net interest income to around $13 billion.
