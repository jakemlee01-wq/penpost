Ardor on the Bluffs wrapped up construction in December 2024, and its owners now have a $50 million bridge loan to carry the property through leasing. Connect CRE and Multifamily & Affordable Housing Business reported that commercial real estate firm Northmarq arranged the financing package for the newly opened Bloomington development.

The deal was negotiated on behalf of owner Endres Holdings by Northmarq's Minneapolis-based debt and equity team, led by Dan Trebil, according to Connect CRE. REBusinessOnline and Traded reported that Endres Holdings, a Hastings-based family office and real estate investment firm, holds the property under the entity Bloomington Apartments, LLC.

According to Northmarq, the package is structured as a three-year bridge loan with extension options, funded through an undisclosed life insurance company. A bridge loan works like a temporary financial trestle, providing short-term capital until permanent borrowing takes over. Trebil stated that ownership and property manager Bader Companies have done a tremendous job, claiming the new debt grants flexibility while the community finishes leasing. Northmarq and Bader Companies reported that the three-year runway gives the team time to stabilize occupancy before securing long-term takeout financing.

The complex was originally planned as a $74 million project developed in partnership with Kaeding Development Group, according to records from Kaeding, Northmarq, and Momentum Design Group. Momentum Design Group designed the building, and Weis Builders constructed it.

Rising six stories at 3701 American Blvd. East in Bloomington's South Loop district, the mid-rise covers about 338,781 square feet, according to Northmarq and Endres Holdings. Connect CRE noted the property sits roughly 11 miles south of downtown Minneapolis, while REBusinessOnline reported direct transit connections to Interstate 494, Highway 77, Minneapolis to St. Paul International Airport, and the Mall of America.

Inside, the 235-unit luxury community offers studio, one-bedroom, two-bedroom, and select three-bedroom layouts, according to Northmarq. Endres Holdings and Northmarq noted that apartments include quartz countertops, built-in desks, patios or balconies, and fireplaces in select units. Shared amenities listed by Northmarq and Bader Companies include an outdoor pool, a spa, a fitness center, underground heated parking, a business center, party rooms, resident lounges, and bluff views overlooking the Minnesota River valley.
