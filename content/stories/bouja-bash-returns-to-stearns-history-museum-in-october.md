AmeriCorps Seniors RSVP will host its annual Bouja Bash fundraiser on Thursday, October 22, 2026, at the Stearns History Museum in St. Cloud. WJON reports the open-house community gathering runs from 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m. at 235 33rd Avenue South.

Tickets are $15 per person. Admission covers all-you-can-eat bouja soup, freshly baked bread, a brownie, and a drink.

Volunteers from Holy Spirit Catholic Church in St. Cloud prepare the stew. According to reporting from KNSI, volunteers chop vegetables by hand for several days and begin cooking the meat as early as 3:00 a.m. on cooking day. Holy Spirit has operated a dedicated bouja shack to brew the soup since roughly 1970, utilizing large steam cooking kettles acquired from the St. Cloud reformatory. The parish uses a recipe originating in the early 1950s, when St. Mary's Cathedral simmered bouja over open fires in its parking lot.

Alongside the meal, the gathering includes live music, bouja trivia, door prize drawings, a silent auction, and access to museum exhibits. WJON reports that attendees can register for pre-event, behind-the-scenes tours of archival collections normally closed to the public by calling 320-650-3083. Take-home containers of frozen bouja will be sold while supplies last.

Ticket sales and proceeds benefit AmeriCorps Seniors RSVP, which matches adults 55 and older with service projects across 160 community and human services agencies in Stearns, Benton, Sherburne, and Wright counties. Last year, 1,200 volunteers contributed more than 140,000 service hours across the region, which equals the labor of 67 full-time staff.

KNSI reported that Granite Financial is serving as the primary sponsor and has pledged to match the first $2,000 raised.
