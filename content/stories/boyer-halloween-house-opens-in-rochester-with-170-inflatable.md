A yard in northwest Rochester is now filled with a record 170 Halloween inflatables as the Boyer Halloween House opens its annual walk-through display. The attraction is located at the Boyer family residence at 5348 51st Street NW, according to reporting by KROC-AM News.

The walk-through officially opened to visitors on Friday, October 16, 2026, following a one-day weather postponement caused by rain. KROC-AM News reported that the display is open to the public every evening from 6:00 p.m. to 9:30 p.m. through November 1, 2026, weather permitting. Along with the paths laid out across the lawn and driveway, the Boyer family worked over the opening weekend to install two spooky scenes inside their garage to expand the experience.

Admission to the display is free, though the family asks visitors for good-will monetary donations to support Channel One Regional Food Bank. People visiting the property can contribute on-site using cash, check, card, or virtually through Venmo. As of Sunday evening, October 18, 2026, the display had collected $1,883.26 for the food bank, according to KROC-AM News.

Mike and Christy Boyer and their family have decorated their home for more than a decade, according to reporting from Rochester Local and Channel One Regional Food Bank. The family began the Halloween tradition in memory of their three-month-old son, Isaac, who passed away from sudden infant death syndrome, or SIDS. Isaac's birthday was close to Halloween.

Channel One Regional Food Bank shared that Christy Boyer saw the annual display as a memorable way to keep Isaac's memory alive. She also noted that while her own family has not needed food shelf services, she previously worked as a social worker and saw firsthand how critical food and nutrition are to people in the community.

The Boyers first began collecting money for Channel One at the display in 2020. In previous years, the annual event has raised more than $23,000 to fight food insecurity across 14 southeastern Minnesota counties.
