On Sunday, October 25, 2026, more than 160 volunteers gathered in the gymnasium at the Brainerd Family YMCA to package 34,560 shelf-stable meals. According to The Outreach Program of Brainerd Lakes and Lakeland News, the event yielded 160 packed cases of food.

The packing day was organized into two 80-person shifts. The morning group packaged 17,280 meals, and the afternoon group matched that output with another 17,280 meals. The packing stations were supervised by 15 Outreach Program volunteer leaders and supported by five student assistants from Brainerd High School.

The volunteer packers came from six area Evangelical Lutheran Church in America congregations. Participating churches included Lord of Life Lutheran Church in Baxter, Crosslake Lutheran Church in Crosslake, Our Savior's Lutheran Church in Pequot Lakes, and three Brainerd congregations: Bethlehem Lutheran Church, Trinity Lutheran Church, and First Lutheran Church.

Volunteers prepared eight different meal varieties, producing 4,320 packets of each. The recipes included Apple Oatmeal, Raisin Oatmeal, Mac and Cheese, Tomato Basil Pasta, Apple Rice, Raisin Rice Pudding, Cheesy Rice, and Spanish Rice. According to The Outreach Program of Brainerd Lakes, each pouch contains six nutrient-dense servings fortified with vitamins, minerals, and protein. Preparing the meals requires three to eight cups of boiling water.

The packaged food is designated for distribution throughout the fall across Central Minnesota. Lakeland News reported that destinations include area food shelves, local shelters, community meal programs, school weekend backpack food programs, and "take-one, need-one" community carts.

The Outreach Program of Brainerd Lakes operates out of Nisswa. Under the nonprofit organization's model, between 80% and 90% of all meals packaged at its events remain within a 60- to 80-mile radius of the Brainerd Lakes area. The remaining 10% to 20% is directed toward broader Minnesota distribution, emergency disaster relief, or international aid.

Lakeland News reported that the organization and its volunteers have packaged more than 5.6 million meals since its founding in 2012. The nonprofit estimates it will package more than 618,000 meals across 2026 to assist households facing food insecurity.
