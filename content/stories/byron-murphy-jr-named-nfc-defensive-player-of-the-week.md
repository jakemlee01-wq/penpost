Byron Murphy Jr. spent his Sunday afternoon in New Orleans collecting footballs, and by Wednesday morning the league office sent back some recognition. The NFL and the Minnesota Vikings announced on Wednesday, Oct. 14, 2026, that Murphy was named NFC Defensive Player of the Week, as reported by Vikings.com and NBC Sports. The award followed Minnesota's 23-13 road victory over the New Orleans Saints on Oct. 11 at the Caesars Superdome, which moved the Vikings to 5-0 on the year.

Murphy intercepted two passes from Saints rookie quarterback Tyler Shough and added a tackle for loss, according to Zone Coverage and AtoZ Sports. Murphy's first pick arrived in the third quarter when Shough scrambled to avoid pass-rush pressure from Eric Wilson and attempted a throw on the run. Murphy tracked the pass and returned the interception 14 yards, according to Vikings.com and NFL.com. His second takeaway came with under three minutes remaining in the fourth quarter when Shough attempted a deep throw intended for receiver Chris Olave and overshot the target.

Describing that late interception, Murphy said he anticipated the route based on the game situation. "Just knowing the situation, we're up, end of game, I knew it was probably gonna be a double move or something like that," Murphy told reporters, according to Vikings.com. "It was a double move; he threw it up and I came down with the ball." Murphy added that the defensive backs knew they had to arrive ready to play. NBC Sports and the Minnesota News Network reported that Murphy finished the contest with two interceptions, three tackles, and one tackle for loss. Behind Murphy's coverage, Minnesota held the Saints to 142 total offensive yards, according to Purple Daily.

The game placed Murphy in recent franchise history. Vikings.com and the Minnesota News Network reported that Murphy is the first Vikings defender to tally two interceptions in a single game since safety Camryn Bynum did so against San Francisco in Week 7 of the 2023 season. The Minnesota News Network noted that Murphy also became the first Viking to record multiple interceptions and a tackle for loss in a game since Harrison Smith did so against Green Bay in Week 16 of the 2017 season.

Those two takeaways elevated Murphy into a tie for the NFL lead with three interceptions on the season, following an interception against Caleb Williams in Week 2, according to Vikings.com and FOX 9. Through five games, Murphy has registered 21 tackles, one tackle for loss, and five passes defensed, FOX 9 reported. His plays brought Minnesota's season total to 10 takeaways, tying them for second in the league. As reported by the Minnesota News Network, the Vikings are the only NFL team to record a takeaway in each of the first five weeks of the 2026 season.

The honor marks Murphy's first weekly award since signing with Minnesota and the second of his career, after winning one with the Arizona Cardinals in 2021, according to AtoZ Sports and Vikings.com. Murphy's recognition represents the 22nd time a Viking has earned a Player of the Week award under head coach Kevin O'Connell, who holds a 48-25 regular-season record in Minnesota.

Murphy's selection gives Minnesota four consecutive weekly NFL honors this season. Vikings.com and the Minnesota News Network reported that the run includes NFC Special Teams Player of the Week awards for Isaiah Rodgers in Week 2, Myles Price in Week 3, and Will Reichard in Week 4. Yahoo Sports, Reddit, and the Minnesota News Network noted that Minnesota matched an NFL record last achieved by the 1991 Buffalo Bills by winning weekly honors in four of the season's first five weeks.

The Vikings entered their Week 6 bye undefeated under O'Connell and defensive coordinator Brian Flores, according to Vikings.com and Zone Coverage. Flores' defense entered the break ranked first across the NFL in points allowed, takeaways, fewest touchdowns surrendered, and opponent third-down conversion rate, according to Zone Coverage. Minnesota returns to play on Sunday, Oct. 25, when they host the Indianapolis Colts at U.S. Bank Stadium, according to Zone Coverage and Columbia Patch.
