After 44 years of square-cut pizzas and dining room fish aquariums, Carbone's Pizzeria in Eagan permanently closed on Saturday, Bring Me The News reported.

The Carbone family first opened the neighborhood establishment back in 1982. Over the decades, the dining room built a following around its pizzas, pastas, and hoagies.

A farewell notice posted on the front door thanked diners for decades of family celebrations and pizza nights. The message offered no specific reason for why the restaurant shut down.

Neighbors holding customer credits or outstanding gift cards will still get their pizza. Those balances will be honored across the river at the original Carbone's location at Maria Avenue and East Seventh Street on St. Paul's East Side.
