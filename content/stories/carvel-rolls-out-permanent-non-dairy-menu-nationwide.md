Carvel has launched a permanent plant-based menu across more than 300 locations nationwide through a partnership with dessert maker KLIMON, according to reporting from QSR Magazine and Dairy Foods.

The move marks an operational shift for the 90-year-old ice cream chain, expanding beyond single alternative items into multi-format plant-based choices across its footprint. According to reporting from QSR Magazine and Big Box Vegan, the permanent rollout follows a 2024 partnership with Oatly that was phased out.

Dairy Foods reported that the new lineup introduces non-dairy options across four core product formats: soft serve, scooped frozen desserts, shakes, and Carvel's trademark Flying Saucers ice cream sandwiches. The menu features nine non-dairy flavors across soft serve and scooped options, with soft serve flavors rotating locally by shop. Customers can also customize their orders with more than 25 non-dairy toppings.

KLIMON, whose name is "no milk" spelled backward, makes 100% plant-based, dairy-free frozen desserts, QSR Magazine and Restaurant Business reported. According to reporting from Big Box Vegan, KLIMON's formulation uses a pea protein and coconut oil base to replicate traditional soft serve consistency, replacing Carvel's earlier oat milk tests.

Carvel announced the rollout on its official channels on Tuesday, October 13, 2026, following more than a year of recipe testing, consumer feedback, and operational development. In statements reported by Dairy Foods, Carvel said it selected KLIMON to deliver the "rich taste, and creamy texture guests expect from the inventors of The Original Soft Serve," adding that the partnership brings "a broader approach to non-dairy innovation that brings Carvel's signature indulgence to more menu occasions."

Before launching the menu nationwide, Carvel piloted KLIMON products in select New York metro shops, including locations in Manhattan, Brooklyn, Ardsley, Bellmore, East Islip, New Rochelle, and Jefferson Valley, Big Box Vegan reported. The permanent menu is now actively available at participating Carvel locations across the United States.
