On Friday, October 23, roughly 4,500 high school students gathered at St. Cloud Technical and Community College for the annual EPIC Student Day, as reported by KNSI. The event brought teenagers from more than 40 regional high schools to the college campus at 1540 Northway Drive in St. Cloud.

EPIC stands for Exploring Potential Interests and Careers, an initiative designed to connect high school education directly to local industry pathways, according to KNSI and EPIC MN. More than 150 regional employers set up interactive work stations and simulations representing approximately 152 distinct careers.

Inside the health science area, students practiced suturing and observed a live birth simulation. Other indoor stations gave students hands-on practice with welding and troubleshooting computer networks, KNSI reported.

For students interested in legal work, court staff from Minnesota's 7th Judicial District Court staged live mock trials throughout the day. In the public safety cluster, students investigated a simulated crime scene, according to KNSI and EPIC MN. Regional grocer Coborn's also ran a grocery-bagging competition.

AgCentric organized several outdoor agriculture and heavy equipment stations. Students at those outdoor displays worked with crop-spraying simulators, conducted soil testing, and watched seed-metering demonstrations.

Greater St. Cloud talent organizers told KNSI that the event aims to keep young workers rooted in Central Minnesota. Organizers structured the exhibits around regional career pipelines, encouraging students to finish their post-secondary education and build their careers in the area.
