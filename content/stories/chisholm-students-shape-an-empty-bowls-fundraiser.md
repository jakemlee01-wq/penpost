Chisholm High School's art department is teaming up with area restaurants to host an Empty Bowls fundraiser to support local families facing hardships, according to reporting from the Mesabi Tribune.

The event takes place from 1:00 p.m. to 6:30 p.m. on Monday, Nov. 9, inside the cafeteria commons of the new Chisholm Elementary School.

Admission and meal tickets are set at $5 for adults ages 18 and older, while children and students 17 and under eat for free. Area restaurants are donating soup and chili for the dinner.

Attendees can also purchase hand-built ceramic bowls crafted by kindergarten through 12th-grade art students. The pieces will sell at four price levels: $1, $5, $10, and $20. In the younger grades, elementary art teacher Sarah Peitso paired her students with high school "Art Buddies" to help guide them through making pottery.

The money raised will be divided between two destinations. Roughly 90% of all proceeds will go directly to the Bluestreak Family Hub, while the remaining 10% stays with the school art department to offset the costs of clay and glaze.

Chisholm School Superintendent Lisa Perkovich said the event gives residents a chance to share a meal, admire student artwork, and ensure neighbors receive needed support.
