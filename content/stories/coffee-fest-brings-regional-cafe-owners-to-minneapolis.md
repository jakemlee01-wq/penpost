More than 1,500 coffee professionals gathered at the Minneapolis Convention Center on October 11 and 12 for Coffee Fest Minneapolis. According to event organizers, the regional trade show brought together more than 120 exhibiting companies. The gathering is a business-to-business convention, meaning it connects suppliers selling equipment, beans, and services directly to shop owners rather than the retail public.

The schedule emphasized the practical side of running independent cafes and roasteries. According to reporting from the Minneapolis Downtown Council, the conference included more than 80 hours of business-focused education and hands-on workshops for operators looking to sharpen their skills. All-access passes covering the full slate of professional classes and entry to the exhibition floor were offered at $455.

Minnesota roasters and suppliers were well represented across the show floor. Exhibitors from around the state included Mill City Roasters, Cafe Imports, Backstory Coffee Roasters, and Misfit Coffee Co., Coffee Fest reported. Local craft also earned peer recognition during the convention, with Duluth Coffee Company receiving the event's Best Consumable Product award for its Peruvian Washed Gesha coffee.
