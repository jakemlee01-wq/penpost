On Monday, October 12, the Minnesota Corn Growers Association advised farmers against putting untaxed, red-dyed off-road diesel into highway vehicles, according to reporting from the Red River Farm Network.

The guidance arrives as machinery expenses mount. According to reporting from Investigate Midwest, on-highway diesel climbed to a national average of $6.20 per gallon on October 5, sharply escalating operating costs for agricultural equipment.

The growers group stated that federal executive action leaves it ambiguous whether highway fuel taxes are being permanently waived or simply deferred, the Red River Farm Network reported. The association cautioned that without clear federal guidance, producers traveling on public roads could face future liability for back taxes or penalties.

The organization announced it is formally petitioning federal officials for explicit rules clarifying fuel tax obligations during the harvest season.
