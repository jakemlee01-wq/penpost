Canadian Pacific Kansas City has released its 2026 Holiday Train schedule, mapping out 25 stops across Minnesota this December. As reported by Bring Me The News and radio station B105, the itinerary adds four stops compared to last year, sending illuminated freight trains to collect food and money for local hunger relief agencies.

According to Railfan & Railroad Magazine and CPKC, the broader international tour runs from Nov. 19 through Dec. 20. The railroad plans to deploy two light-covered trains across 182 communities in Canada and the United States over 32 days. Radio station B105 reported that each train stretches roughly 1,000 feet long and is decorated with hundreds of thousands of LED lights. One modified boxcar features a fold-down side panel that converts into a mobile stage for free 30-minute community concerts.

The charitable initiative began in 1999 under Canadian Pacific and is now in its 28th year. According to reporting from B105 and Bring Me The News, the program has raised more than $28.3 million and gathered over 5.6 million pounds of food to date. CPKC makes a corporate cash donation to a local food shelf at every scheduled stop. B105 and Rochester Local noted that attendees are encouraged to bring nonperishable food items or monetary gifts, with all collections remaining in the community where the stop occurs. In Golden Valley, city officials and Ingstad Media confirmed that donations will go to the PRISM food shelf.

The Minnesota leg begins Dec. 11 at 1:15 p.m. in Rochester, according to Bring Me The News and B105. Twin Cities stops open on the evening of Friday, Dec. 12, when the train arrives at the CPKC depot at 500 E. Second St. in Hastings from 8:45 to 9:15 p.m.

On Saturday, Dec. 13, the train makes an East Metro stop in Cottage Grove at 7064 W. Point Douglas Rd. at 5:15 p.m. It then moves to St. Paul's Union Depot at 214 E. 4th St. for a 7:00 p.m. concert.

On Sunday, Dec. 14, the tour heads into the West Metro. The train stops at the Golden Hills Drive crossing in Golden Valley at 5:00 p.m., followed by a 6:15 p.m. stop in St. Louis Park at Lake Street and Liberty Lane. Minneapolis hosts the final metro stop of that evening at 8:15 p.m. at 37th Avenue and Stinson Boulevard. After traveling through central Minnesota, the train visits western Hennepin County on Dec. 16, stopping at the County Road 19 railway crossing in Loretto at 4:30 p.m.

Grammy-nominated soul and pop singer-songwriter Allen Stone will perform the free half-hour concerts from the train car stage through the Dec. 14 Minneapolis stop. Bring Me The News reported that musicians Shawnee Kish and Jamie Fine will take over the stage for the rest of the Minnesota tour. The state itinerary concludes on Dec. 18 in Elbow Lake before the train crosses west into North Dakota.
