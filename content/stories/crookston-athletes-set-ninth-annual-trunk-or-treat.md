The University of Minnesota Crookston Student-Athlete Advisory Committee is preparing its ninth annual community Trunk-or-Treat event, local station KROX reported.

Scheduled for Wednesday, October 28, the outdoor gathering will run from 6:00 p.m. to 7:15 p.m. along the UMC Campus Mall. Admission is free for all visiting families, children, and community members.

Visitors arriving by car have designated parking in the lot adjacent to the Kiehle Building, which sits directly west of the campus mall.

Roughly 55 student-athletes on the advisory committee are coordinating and staffing the event this school year. Nearly all of Minnesota Crookston's 16 intercollegiate varsity athletic programs plan to take part by stationing decorated cars across the grounds and distributing candy.

Each participating athletic program and campus student club is invited to decorate one vehicle with Halloween themes and hand out treats directly from the trunk.

According to RJ Broadcasting, the gathering serves as a safe trick-or-treating alternative that highlights school spirit, festive decorations, and an appearance by a costumed mascot. Hunter Plante, the university's assistant athletic director of athletic operations, coordinated the announcement, according to UMC Golden Eagle Athletics.

The event has an established turnout to match, with KROX reporting that the 2025 celebration brought out more than 30 clubs and Golden Eagle teams. That previous gathering drew an estimated crowd of more than 450 trick-or-treaters to the Crookston campus.
