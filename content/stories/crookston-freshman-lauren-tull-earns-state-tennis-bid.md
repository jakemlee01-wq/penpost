Five matches in two days can make an indoor tennis court feel like an endless loop of squeaking sneakers and bright yellow fuzz. Crookston High School freshman Lauren Tull powered through that marathon to earn a trip to the state tournament.

According to reporting from radio station KROX, Tull qualified for the Class A tournament after winning a true-second match at the Section 8A Individual Girls Tennis Tournament, which wrapped up on Saturday, October 17, 2026, indoors at Choice Fitness in Grand Forks.

Tull stepped onto the courts as the tournament's No. 2 seed, carrying a 27-7 regular-season record. She opened play on Friday by sweeping No. 14 seed Emma Wright of Roseau without dropping a single game, winning 6-0, 6-0. In the quarterfinals, Tull handled No. 7 seed Hattie Truax of Parkers Prairie in straight sets, 6-4, 6-1.

Saturday morning brought a battle of endurance in the semifinals against fellow freshman and No. 3 seed Haley Larson of Park Rapids. The first set alone stretched across approximately 90 minutes, resembling a stubborn tug-of-war until Tull pulled out a 7-6 tiebreaker win. She quickly took the second set 6-1 to earn a berth in the section championship.

The title match pitted Tull against Staples-Motley senior Ronnie Noska, the top seed who entered ranked fifth in Class A with a 28-4 record. Noska won 6-1, 6-0 to capture the section title and clinch her fifth career singles trip to state.

Because Noska had earlier defeated Roseau senior Elizabeth Desrosier, Section 8A rules required a true-second match between Tull and Desrosier to settle the final qualifying spot. Tull bounced back immediately, defeating Desrosier 6-1, 6-2 to secure the runner-up state bid. The win pushed Tull's overall season record to 28-8.

Postseason tennis trips run in the family. Tull's mother, Jennifer (Coauette) Tull, competed at state for the Crookston team that finished fourth in 1993, and her older sister, Brekken Tull, reached the state team tournament twice during her high school career.

The individual run followed a quick turnaround from the team playoffs under head coach Brianne Ellison. Crookston's team season ended on Thursday, October 15, with a 6-1 first-round section loss to Park Rapids, where Tull delivered the Pirates' lone victory.

Tull now advances to the Minnesota State High School League Class A Individual State Tournament as the Section 8A runner-up. Competition begins at 8:00 a.m. on Thursday, October 29, 2026, at the Reed-Sweatt Family Tennis Center in Minneapolis.
