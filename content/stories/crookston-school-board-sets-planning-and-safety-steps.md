The Crookston School Board approved an agreement for strategic planning services and applied for high school athletic grants during its regular meeting on Monday, October 26, 2026. The meeting took place at 5:00 p.m. in the large group room at Highland Elementary School, local radio station KROX reported.

The strategic planning contract brings in the Minnesota School Boards Association to help map out long-term priorities for the district. Initial discussions started the prior Friday during a workshop led by Barb Dorn, the association's director of leadership development and executive search. During that session, board members pointed to financial stability and steady administrative leadership as their primary goals. The board scheduled a working session for Thursday, October 29, 2026, at 1:00 p.m. to keep developing the plan.

Board members also approved two grant resolutions through the Minnesota State High School League for the 2026 to 2027 school year. Form A requests grant funding to lower student activity participation fees. Form B asks for $5,373 to buy three automated external defibrillators, portable medical devices that deliver an electric shock to restore a regular heart rhythm, and to fund CPR training for coaches. The league finances these grants through state sales taxes collected on ticket sales at state tournament events.

Interim Superintendent Todd Selk presented the athletic grant forms on behalf of Activities Director Evan Pederson, who was absent. Selk said the new defibrillators will replace aging devices or add coverage across district athletic facilities, including gymnasiums, the football field, and baseball and softball diamonds. Selk stated that district officials hope they do not have to use them, but need to be prepared if the situation comes up.

Personnel changes also drew formal board action. The board accepted the resignation of Wendy Ault, the district's human resources director and payroll specialist, whose last day was Friday, October 23, 2026. Selk expressed surprise over her departure, thanked her for her service, and noted she had been helpful during his transition. To maintain bi-weekly payroll operations without interruption, the board approved an agreement with Denise Seberson to serve as interim payroll accountant.

The board approved several new hires, including Steven Howey as a full-time substitute teacher at Crookston High School, paraprofessionals Faith Hanson and Mariah Kurz, Highland lunchroom aide Lenore Anderson, and Highland playground and lunchroom supervisor Jennifer Austreng. The board also approved the winter coaches list. In other staffing actions, the board accepted the resignations of Highland Elementary paraprofessionals Ashley Powell and Karley Pearson.

On the financial side, the board approved payment of district bills and accounts totaling $1,609,109.29. The board also formally accepted $20,544.10 in local donations. Those contributions included $8,352 from the Pirate Fine Arts Boosters for music and art equipment, $8,000 from the Pirate Girls Hockey Club, $2,442.10 from the Crookston Pirate Boosters for wrestling singlets, $1,500 from the Crookston FFA Alumni for National Convention expenses, and $250 from the Crookston Community Theatre for the Valley Fiddlers.

With three board seats appearing on the November ballot, members added a statutory reorganizational meeting for Monday, January 4, 2027, at 5:00 p.m. to swear in incoming members. The next regular monthly school board meeting is set for Monday, November 23, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. in the choir and orchestra room at Crookston High School.
