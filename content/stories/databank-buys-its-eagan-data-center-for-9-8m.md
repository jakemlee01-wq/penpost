Dallas-based DataBank is trading rent for ownership in Eagan, purchasing its existing 5-megawatt carrier hotel facility for more than $9.8 million, according to the Minneapolis / St. Paul Business Journal. A carrier hotel is a specialized hub where different telecommunications networks link together.

The property encompasses 48,860 square feet of space. The transaction officially closed on Sept. 30, 2026, according to Minneapolis Department of Revenue records.

The seller was an LLC affiliated with Singapore-based Mapletree Investments, the Business Journal reported. DataBank executed the purchase as part of an overarching initiative to acquire facilities it previously leased, according to the outlet.
