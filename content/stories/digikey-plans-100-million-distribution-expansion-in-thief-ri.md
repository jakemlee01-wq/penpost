DigiKey is spending more than $100 million over the next 18 months to expand its distribution center in Thief River Falls, according to reporting from The Mighty 790 KFGO and the Thief River Falls Times. The company announced the multi-year project on Monday, October 12, 2026, as reported by KFGO and WiLD 102 Radio.

The investment will add automated order retrieval systems, expanded picking capacity, advanced packing operations, and an overhaul of DigiKey's global order management technology platform. Capital investment means money spent on physical equipment and facilities rather than routine operating costs.

The work will build into the upper floors of DigiKey’s 2.2 million-square-foot product distribution facility, which opened in August 2022. KFGO reported that the building was intentionally designed with open space on its fourth floor to accommodate future automation and scaling.

According to KFGO and the Thief River Falls Times, the first new automated packing system is already on-site in Pennington County and will become operational in the coming months. Additional automation equipment will be phased in over the next several years.

The installation comes alongside large revenue projections from the company. DigiKey reported that it expects annual revenue to grow by more than 50% in 2026, representing $1.5 billion in incremental revenue this year alone, according to KFGO and WiLD 102 Radio. The company projects an additional $1 billion in revenue growth for 2027.

The Thief River Falls Times reported that those projected revenue gains across 2026 and 2027 represent the economic equivalent of adding two new billion-dollar companies to northwest Minnesota.

From its headquarters in northwest Minnesota, DigiKey currently inventories and distributes more than 17.4 million electronic components and automation products to over 1 million customers worldwide, according to KFGO.

Chief Executive Officer Dave Doherty addressed the project in comments reported by KFGO and the Thief River Falls Times. "This community built DigiKey, and this investment is about building DigiKey for the next decade, not just the next quarter," Doherty said. "We're all in on growth for our customers around the world, and for the people who make it happen every day right here in Thief River Falls."

Doherty also told the Thief River Falls Times that the upgrades show customers, suppliers, and neighbors that the company is not slowing down. Doherty noted, "While our communication around investments places a high focus on technology, that has never been the key to DigiKey's success. Technology can be purchased by any company."
