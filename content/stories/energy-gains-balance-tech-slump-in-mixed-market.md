Stocks traded mixed on October 12 as gains in energy shares countered a slump in technology companies ahead of an upcoming inflation report. According to reporting from the Associated Press, the Dow Jones Industrial Average rose 0.4% on Monday, October 12.

Higher oil prices provided momentum for energy companies. Brent crude climbed 2.1% to $106.95 per barrel amid Middle East supply risks, the Associated Press reported. Those market moves helped lift Exxon Mobil shares by 1.8%, while Chevron advanced between 1.4% and 1.9%.

Technology shares moved downward during the session. The Associated Press reported that Micron Technology fell between 2.8% and 4.2%. In the bond market, Charles Schwab reported that the 10-year Treasury yield, which represents the interest rate on long-term government debt, finished Friday at 5.23% to 5.24%.
