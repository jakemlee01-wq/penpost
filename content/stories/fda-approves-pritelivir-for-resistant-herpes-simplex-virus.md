On Oct. 9, 2026, the U.S. Food and Drug Administration approved pritelivir tablets, an oral antiviral marketed as Hovilpri by Asahi Kasei Therapeutics. Pharmacy Times reported on the regulatory decision on Oct. 12.

The drug is cleared for immunocompromised adult patients suffering from mucocutaneous herpes simplex virus infections that do not respond to first-line therapies. It treats refractory cases where standard care regimens, including acyclovir, valacyclovir, or famciclovir, have failed.

In a review of the drug's safety profile, pharmacists noted that co-administration with acid-reducing medications can decrease pritelivir exposure. They noted that this drug interaction can reduce the treatment's therapeutic efficacy.
