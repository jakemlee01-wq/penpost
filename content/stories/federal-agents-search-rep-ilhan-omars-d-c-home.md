Agents from Homeland Security Investigations executed a court-authorized federal search warrant at the Washington, D.C., residence of Democratic U.S. Rep. Ilhan Omar on the morning of Monday, October 12, 2026, according to The Associated Press.

The Washington Post reported that the criminal inquiry focuses on immigration paperwork completed decades ago by Omar's late father after her family fled Somalia as refugees in the 1990s. The outlet also reported that Omar released a statement declaring she will not be silenced, calling the search an abuse of power and political intimidation.

House Democratic leaders and Minnesota Gov. Tim Walz issued a joint condemnation denouncing the federal operation, according to CBS News and WCCO.

In Minnesota, the state chapter of the Council on American-Islamic Relations scheduled a community solidarity rally for Monday afternoon at the Powderhorn Park Recreation Center in Minneapolis, according to PBS News.
