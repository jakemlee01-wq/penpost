Special agents with Homeland Security Investigations executed a court-authorized search warrant at the Washington, D.C.-area residence of Rep. Ilhan Omar on Monday morning, October 12, 2026, The Washington Post reported.

The U.S. Department of Justice confirmed the warrant is connected to an active federal criminal inquiry focusing on immigration documentation submitted by Omar's late father, according to CBS News. Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin previously acknowledged the active federal inquiry during public comments last month, PBS News reported.

Omar issued a statement calling the search an attempt to target political enemies and weaponize federal law enforcement, The Washington Post reported. House Democratic leadership also released a joint statement denouncing the action as a rogue abuse of power ahead of midterm elections, according to The Guardian.
