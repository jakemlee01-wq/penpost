The City of Fergus Falls has started formal annexation proceedings for unincorporated land just outside its borders to make room for a development known as Project Glacier, according to reporting from Fergus Now.

Project Glacier is a proposed logistics and distribution facility planned to handle commercial shipping across a multistate market. Development planners estimate the facility will bring up to 150 permanent jobs to Fergus Falls. Groundbreaking is tentatively scheduled for April 2027.

Bringing the unincorporated property into city limits is required before municipal staff can extend public utilities, complete site plan reviews, update zoning, and coordinate local transportation infrastructure. City officials launched the annexation process in late 2026 to work through administrative steps well ahead of the planned construction date.

The corporate identity of the prospective tenant has not been disclosed publicly due to ongoing private development negotiations, Fergus Now reported.

Fergus Falls Mayor Anthony Hicks said the project represents a significant jobs and investment opportunity, noting that up to 150 jobs would have a tremendous impact on the community and the surrounding region. Hicks stated that the city's current focus is making sure Fergus Falls is ready, protecting the private development process, and keeping the project on schedule.

City Administrator Andrew Bremseth explained that substantial preparation must happen before a facility of this size can break ground. Bremseth said beginning the annexation process allows the city to maintain the April 2027 construction timeline while following the proper public process.

The annexation will move through mandated public notices, statutory review periods, and public hearings before the Fergus Falls City Council for review and final consideration. City officials noted that further specifications and company details will be shared as development agreements advance.
