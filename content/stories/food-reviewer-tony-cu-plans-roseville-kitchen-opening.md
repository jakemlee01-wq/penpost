Social media food reviewer Tony Cu announced plans to open his first brick-and-mortar restaurant, Tony Cu’s, inside the B-Dale Club in Roseville, according to reporting by Bring Me The News.

The restaurant is scheduled to open to the public on Dec. 1. Cu is leasing the commercial kitchen at the venue, a long-running nonprofit community social club located at 2100 Dale Street North.

Cu built a large digital following across the Midwest, accumulating nearly 750,000 followers on TikTok alone, Bring Me The News reported. His signature review format involves ordering takeout dishes recommended by his followers, eating them from the front seat of his car outside restaurants across Minnesota and the country, and offering unfiltered reviews alongside recipe videos.

WJON reported that Cu first started posting food content online in April 2023. His online audience grew quickly after he shared home recipes for egg rolls and fried rice.

Now Cu is moving from reviewing other kitchens to running one. Tony Cu’s will operate seven days a week, serving both lunch and dinner. Bring Me The News reported that the baseline menu will feature standard club food, including burgers, wings, wraps, and salads.

The kitchen also plans to serve rotating homestyle and cultural specials. Those planned rotating items include meatloaf, Vietnamese pho, and egg rolls.

Cu acknowledged the scale of the undertaking in his announcement. "I'm excited for this. I know it's going to be a lot of work, but the next two months will be quite exciting and I can't wait to share that with you guys," Cu said.
