A three-alarm fire tore through two connected commercial buildings at 1300 and 1308 East Franklin Avenue in Minneapolis on Sunday afternoon, collapsing the roofs and injuring two firefighters.

The Minneapolis Fire Department was dispatched at 4:03 p.m. on Sunday, Oct. 11, according to Bring Me The News and the fire department. While crews were inside, heavy shelving collapsed onto hose lines and blocked an exit, prompting a Mayday call before the firefighters evacuated.

Two firefighters suffered steam burns and heat exhaustion, Bring Me The News and MPR News reported. Hennepin County Medical Center paramedics treated them at the scene, and both were transported to a hospital and released. Crews continued fighting the three-alarm fire into Sunday night as the roofs gave way.

The fire also threatened neighboring facilities housing irreplaceable Native American records, according to FOX 9. Those facilities hold what the executive director of Wicoie Nandagikaden described as Minnesota's oldest Dakota language archive.
