Mankato police officers responded around 12:30 a.m. on Sunday, October 11, to a reported physical altercation in the 500 block of South Front Street, according to FOX 9.

During a physical struggle to take the suspect into custody, the suspect's gun fired, FOX 9 reported. No one was injured by the gunfire, and officers never discharged their service weapons.

Radio Mankato reported that the suspect was identified as 29-year-old Justice Arness Tryon of St. Paul.

Tryon was arrested and taken to a local hospital for a precautionary medical evaluation, according to FOX 9, with formal charges pending.
