For more than 20 years, HmongTown Marketplace on Como Avenue in St. Paul has been a place where new businesses get their start. Now its future is in front of a judge.

Stearns Bank, based in St. Cloud, sued founder Toua Xiong in mid-September, seeking about $6.6 million in loans, interest and late fees, according to reporting by Sahan Journal published by MPR News. The bank filed for mortgage foreclosure and asked the court to appoint a receiver, an outside manager who would take over running the property, including collecting rent. A Hennepin County judge is scheduled to consider that request on Nov. 24.

The bank alleges that loans on the marketplace and on a former Sears building in Maplewood, which Xiong bought in 2023 for $5.25 million, went into default in 2025. Xiong has filed counterclaims, including that the bank promised him a construction loan for the Sears site and didn't follow through. He says he used marketplace income to pay the Sears loan, which delayed repairs at the market. These are each side's claims. The bank declined to comment, and Xiong's attorney says the two sides are discussing a resolution without a trial.

Xiong opened the market in 2004 with more than 200 vendors. Some vendors say customer traffic dropped after news of the lawsuit spread, along with rumors that the market was closing. No closure has been announced. The next step is the November hearing.
