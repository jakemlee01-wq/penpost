Alexandria has 92 new hotel rooms right across the street from Cub Foods. The Alexandria Lakes Area Chamber of Commerce hosted an official ribbon-cutting ceremony and public open house on Tuesday, October 13, 2026, to celebrate the new Holiday Inn Express & Suites at 2673 S. Broadway St.

According to the chamber and the Douglas County Community Calendar, the ribbon cutting took place at 5:00 p.m. The ceremony was paired with a two-hour public open house running from 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., giving community members and visitors an opportunity to tour the newly constructed building. In its announcement, the chamber stated that everyone was welcome to attend.

The newly built, three-story property actually finished construction and began checking in guests on August 14, 2026, according to reporting from Travel Weekly and Explore Alexandria, MN. In addition to facing Cub Foods, the building sits adjacent to the Hampton Inn & Broadway Ballroom.

The IHG Hotels & Resorts property features 92 guestrooms and suites. Travel Weekly and Explore Alexandria, MN report that rooms include plush bedding, microwaves, and mini-refrigerators, with overnight rates typically running between $99 and $299. Guests traveling with animals can bring pets weighing up to 75 pounds for a $35 nightly fee.

For travelers balancing work and rest, the property includes a 24/7 business center, guest meeting space, and a 200-capacity event and conference room. Visitors also have access to free high-speed Wi-Fi, an indoor swimming pool, on-site electric vehicle charging stations, and a dedicated fitness center that remains open from 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.

Explore Alexandria, MN and IHG Hotels & Resorts note that morning stays include a complimentary "Express Start" breakfast. The layout features bean-to-cup coffee, hot buffet items, cinnamon rolls, and an automated pancake machine.

The property sits 1.4 miles south of downtown Alexandria, based on records from IHG and Klook. That puts the hotel within a five-minute drive of Alomere Health hospital, and 1.9 miles away from both the Runestone Museum and the Minnesota Lakes Maritime Museum.
