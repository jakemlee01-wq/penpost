St. Paul-based nonprofit HOURCAR announced its board of directors voted to shut down all operations at noon on Thursday, October 15, 2026, as reported by Bring Me The News and Axios.

The closure brings an end to two local transportation services, according to Bring Me The News: the 20-year-old station-based HOURCAR fleet and the Evie all-electric point-to-point network. Across Minneapolis and St. Paul, the shutdown affects more than 4,000 active car-sharing members, the Minneapolis / St. Paul Business Journal and Bring Me The News reported.

Organization leaders cited an immediate funding shortfall of roughly $625,000 alongside an overall $1.1 million budget deficit, according to reporting from the Minneapolis / St. Paul Business Journal and Racket. Leaders stated that nationwide spikes in auto insurance rates and cuts to federal support caused the financial gap.

Following the announcement, community organizers scheduled an emergency rally at Government Plaza in downtown Minneapolis for 6:00 p.m. on Tuesday, October 13, 2026.
