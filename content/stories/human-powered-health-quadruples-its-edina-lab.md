Elite athletic testing usually stays behind closed doors, but Human Powered Health has built its business around offering clinical assessments directly to the public. Now, the health startup has relocated its corporate headquarters and flagship lab to The Craftsman on France at 7250 France Ave. S. in Edina, the Minneapolis / St. Paul Business Journal reported.

The move expands the performance lab to more than 10,000 square feet, quadrupling the footprint of the original Edina site that opened in April 2023 at 50th and France. In addition to the lab floor, the company leased approximately 7,500 square feet of office space in the building to house its headquarters staff.

Dan Cohen, chief performance officer and the company's second employee, told the Minneapolis / St. Paul Business Journal that the company had simply run out of room at 3925 W. 50th St. Cohen said the previous corporate office was crowded and multiple people were arriving at once for appointments, creating the need for a larger footprint. The new setup allows the facility to handle three to four times more visitors per day than the earlier location, Cohen reported.

Dave Wichmann, the former chief executive officer of Minnetonka-based UnitedHealth Group, founded Human Powered Health in 2022. While the company provides specialized tests like bike fittings and force plate movement analysis, elite competitors are not the primary customer. The Minneapolis / St. Paul Business Journal reported that approximately 70% of clients are everyday consumers, including corporate executives, employer wellness participants, and older adults.

Client volume has more than doubled year over year, supported by a partner network of roughly 150 medical concierge, rehabilitation, and sports organizations. The new lab includes 1,750 square feet of dedicated turf space to facilitate running and athletic performance testing. Visitors can also receive dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry, or DXA scans, to measure bone density and body composition, resting metabolic rate tests, running gait analysis, and VO2 max assessments to measure aerobic capacity.

Force plate testing is used to measure balance and symmetry. All testing, data interpretation, and personalized health recommendations are run by master's- and PhD-level performance physiologists and data scientists. In remarks reported by PR Newswire, Cohen noted that the startup is building a model that connects objective physiological data with individual action as consumers look for measurable approaches to longevity and prevention.

Human Powered Health shares The Craftsman on France with other commercial tenants, including Charles Schwab Corp. and Adolfson & Peterson Construction, the Minneapolis / St. Paul Business Journal reported. The Edina lab operates by appointment Monday through Friday from 6:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., and Saturday from 7:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m.
