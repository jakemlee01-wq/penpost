A quick shot from the bottom of the left faceoff circle lifted the Minnesota Wild to a 2-1 victory over the Columbus Blue Jackets on Sunday, October 18, 2026, at Grand Casino Arena in downtown St. Paul.

Kirill Kaprizov broke a 1-1 tie on a power play at 3:06 of the second period by beating Columbus goaltender Cam Talbot over the shoulder, The Associated Press reported. That shot was Kaprizov's 10th goal of the season in just nine games. According to reporting from FOX 9, NHL Public Relations, and Heavy Sports, it set a franchise record for the fastest player to reach double-digit regular-season goals, surpassing Brian Rolston, who hit 10 goals in 12 games during the 2006-07 season.

FOX 9 and Hockey.com noted that Kaprizov is the first player across the NHL to reach 10 goals during the 2026-27 campaign. NHL Public Relations reported that he is also just the sixth active NHL player to hit double digits in nine games or fewer, joining Auston Matthews, Alex Ovechkin, David Pastrnak, Nikita Kucherov, and Sidney Crosby.

The score was Kaprizov's 240th career regular-season NHL goal, extending his scoring streak to goals in three straight games and points in six consecutive games, according to Hockey.com and the NHL Russian Goal Archive. Kaprizov has totaled seven goals and five assists during that six-game stretch.

FOX 9 reported that Maxim Shabanov and center Joel Eriksson Ek assisted on the power-play winner. That secondary assist gave Eriksson Ek 200 career NHL assists, making him the 12th player in Wild franchise history to reach that milestone.

Minnesota opened the scoring late in the first period. The Associated Press and Hockey.com reported that forward Danila Yurov converted a centering pass from Yakov Trenin on an odd-man rush at 18:12 of the opening frame. Exactly 60 seconds later, Columbus answered during a delayed penalty. Adam Fantilli scored on a slap shot through traffic from the right circle, with assists from Zach Werenski and Sean Monahan.

Goaltender Jesper Wallstedt earned his fourth straight win for Minnesota, turning aside 13 of 14 shots. His night included an early breakaway save against Connor Garland two minutes into the contest, The Associated Press reported. Columbus netminder Talbot stopped 32 of 34 shots, which included three saves during a Minnesota five-on-three power play that lasted 1:52.

The win came while Minnesota skated without two top defensemen, FOX 9 reported. Brock Faber was out, and team captain Jared Spurgeon was placed on injured reserve with an upper-body injury.

Even with those absences, FOX 9 noted that the victory improved the Central Division-leading Wild to 6-2-1 on the season. The team remains perfect on home ice at 4-0-0. That matches the club's best home start since the 2016-17 season and marks their eighth consecutive regular-season home victory dating back to last year.

The Wild now head out for a five-game road trip, starting Tuesday night against the Edmonton Oilers, The Associated Press reported. The Blue Jackets fell to 3-4-0 and return home to host the Toronto Maple Leafs on Tuesday.
