The National Weather Service in Duluth placed Lake Superior nearshore and open waters under a Gale Warning on Monday, Oct. 12, 2026. Gale-force winds across the water generated waves exceeding 10 feet on the open lake.

During the peak of the storm system, forecasters projected wave heights would climb between 12 and 18 feet in exposed areas. The conditions officially marked the arrival of the autumn gale season for maritime traffic across western Lake Superior, Northern News Now reported.

Inland, the passing low-pressure front brought widespread rain showers and cooler weather to Duluth, sending local temperatures down into the 40s and low 50s.
