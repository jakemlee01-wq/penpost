Leighton Media is acquiring radio station KMGK (107.1 FM) in Glenwood for $160,000, according to filings reported by NorthPine, Upper Midwest Broadcasting, and the Radio & Television Business Report. The station is being sold by Steven Nestor’s company, Branstock Communications, which has owned the west-central Minnesota outlet since 1993.

An asset purchase agreement submitted to the Federal Communications Commission on Oct. 13, 2026, outlines the transaction. Leighton Radio Holdings is acquiring KMGK's intellectual property, while Leighton Enterprises is buying the station's physical real estate and related assets. The Radio & Television Business Report noted that Leighton submitted a $32,000 escrow deposit, which is funds held by a third party until closing conditions are met, with the remainder due when the sale closes. Attorney John Wells King served as legal counsel, and Nestor signed off as president after serving as general manager throughout Branstock's ownership.

Pending final FCC approval of the license transfer, Leighton is scheduled to take over day-to-day operations and programming on Nov. 1, 2026, under a local marketing agreement, a contract allowing one broadcaster to manage programming and advertising on another company's station.

Branded on air as "Magic 107.1" and "Smooth Magic," KMGK operates at 3,300 watts of effective radiated power, a technical measure of transmitter signal strength, from the shores of Lake Minnewaska in Glenwood. NorthPine and Upper Midwest Broadcasting reported that the station airs a classic hits format, a nightly block of Old Time Radio, high school athletics, and Minnesota Twins games. Its signal primarily reaches rural communities south of Alexandria across Pope and Douglas counties, including Starbuck, Alexandria, Cyrus, and Morris.

NorthPine reported that KMGK's city-grade signal coverage does not overlap with Leighton's existing Alexandria transmitters, which include KXRA (1490 AM), KXRA-FM (92.3 FM), and KXRZ (99.3 FM). Glenwood is already served by Leighton-owned translator K289CL (105.7 FM), which rebroadcasts KXRA.

Headquartered in St. Cloud, Leighton Media runs station clusters across Minnesota and eastern North Dakota, with locations in Alexandria, Detroit Lakes, Fergus Falls, Perham, St. Cloud, Winona, and Grand Forks. The Glenwood deal follows an agreement Leighton made in late September 2026 to buy out five major shareholders for $7.84 million, shifting full ownership into an Employee Stock Ownership Plan, a program that allocates company shares to workers.
