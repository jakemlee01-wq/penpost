Little Falls Community Schools is celebrating the 50th anniversary of the Little Falls High School Indoor Marching Band Show this fall, AM 1240 WJON reported. The district issued an open call inviting all Flyer Band alumni from the past five decades to return and perform in the milestone concert.

The 50th annual indoor marching band show is scheduled for a two-night run on November 23 and November 24 at Little Falls Community High School. Band alumni interested in playing were asked to register by Thursday so organizers could finalize performance arrangements and musical assignments for the November dates.

All three band directors who have led the program across its 50-year history will attend the event. Dwight Nelson, Jonathan Laflamme, and current director Todd Peterson are all scheduled to be in attendance for the celebration.

Peterson has been directing the program for nine years, according to AM 1240 WJON. He compared the concert to a theatrical production, noting that there are many moving pieces and that students deserve two opportunities to perform it.

The concert format mirrors a variety show, with students taking an active role in shaping the lineup by picking many of their own pieces. Student sections select and feature their own music alongside pep band tunes, classic hits, and modern rock. Peterson noted that the musical selections are chosen so audience members will recognize the songs and be able to sing along.

Peterson said students have welcomed the two-night schedule because it gives them multiple chances to perform after all that preparation. Across his nine years leading the ensemble, Peterson said he has never heard a student complain about doing two nights, adding that the musicians enjoy playing both evenings.
