The 17th annual Mankato Marathon brought roughly 3,600 runners to downtown Mankato on October 16 and 17, 2026. According to reporting from KEYC News Now, participants arrived representing 34 states and six countries across the weekend's slate of races.

For the first time in the event's 17-year history, organizers introduced a redesigned point-to-point course for the full marathon, half marathon, relay, and 10K. MarathonGuide.com and Visit Mankato reported that the purpose-built layout was designed to be faster and flatter than past iterations. The change moved the starting line to the Minnesota State University, Mankato campus near the Maverick All-Sports Dome at 1910 Monks Avenue, directing runners to a finish line on South Second Street in downtown Mankato.

Because the redesigned route ran point to point, morning shuttle buses transported participants from Cherry and South Front streets downtown up to the campus start line. Planning the route required coordination between a volunteer task force, the City of Mankato, Blue Earth County, and the university.

The 26.2-mile course remained an official Boston Marathon qualifier, which means runners can use their certified times to qualify for the Boston race. Timing was handled by Anderson Race Management, and KEYC News Now reported that the full route carried runners past the Red Jacket Trail and through the Minnesota River Valley. Mayo Clinic Health System served as the title and presenting sponsor, providing on-course medical personnel and post-race stretching.

Joy Leafblad told KEYC News Now that runners were excited about the new course, and stated that the event alone delivered an estimated economic impact of more than $3 million to the Greater Mankato community.

The weekend schedule opened Friday at the Mayo Clinic Health System Event Center at 1 Civic Center Plaza, hosting the Scheels Wellness Expo from noon to 6:00 p.m. Running events began Friday evening with the KidsK at 4:30 p.m. and the Pork Power 5K at 5:30 p.m. departing from 417 Poplar Street. Visit Mankato noted that while the longer distance races shifted to the university campus, the 5K and youth runs kept their traditional route through Sibley Park.

Saturday's program launched at 7:20 a.m. with the full marathon and marathon relay, followed by the Monarch Healthcare Management 10K and the half marathon. In the half marathon, Josiah Swanson won the men's overall title with a time of 1:09:52, and Laura Reed finished first in the women's division with a time of 1:25:04.

Events wrapped up with a post-race street gathering along the 500 block of South Front Street. Race officials awarded top-three male, female, and co-ed finishers in the marathon, half marathon, relay, and 10K custom trophies produced by local quarry operator Vetter Stone, while 5K division winners received gift baskets from the Minnesota Pork Producers.
