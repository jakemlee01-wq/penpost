Wisconsin manufacturer Preco has signed a long-term lease to relocate its operations across the state border to Vadnais Heights, according to reporting by the Minneapolis / St. Paul Business Journal.

The relocation is projected to bring more than 100 manufacturing and operational jobs into Minnesota. The company plans to occupy an 80,000-square-foot space at the Vadnais Heights Business Center, located at 980 Berwood Ave. E., with the move scheduled for completion in early 2027.

Preco is currently based in Somerset, Wisconsin. The business specializes in advanced material-processing equipment and contract manufacturing services, which involves producing components or finished goods for other companies under agreement. Outside of its Midwest operations, Preco runs an additional U.S. facility in Lenexa, Kansas.

Preco Interim President Luke Schraw stated that the new facility gives the company an opportunity to support employees, strengthen operations, and position itself for continued growth, the Minneapolis / St. Paul Business Journal reported.

The transaction brings the 100,000-square-foot Vadnais Heights building to full occupancy, according to property owner Endeavor Development. The remaining space in the complex is leased to plastics fabricator Cope Plastics Inc., which is based in Alton, Illinois.

The 80,000-square-foot space was previously leased by food-packaging company Nextera Packaging. Endeavor Development built a separate facility at Park 35E in White Bear Township, and Nextera relocated to that site before Preco took over the vacancy on Berwood Avenue.

Edina-based Endeavor purchased the Vadnais Heights property, originally built in 1998, for $10.5 million in 2024. The firm confirmed the lease agreement on Monday, Oct. 12, 2026.

The industrial building includes a 24-foot clear ceiling height, 16 loading dock doors, two drive-in doors, and 181 vehicle parking stalls, according to the Minneapolis / St. Paul Business Journal.

Kris Smeltzer of Cushman & Wakefield represented Preco in the lease negotiations. Cetra Partners reported that broker Jay Chmieleski represented Endeavor Development.
