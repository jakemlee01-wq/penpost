A public candidate forum for southwest Minnesota legislative contenders is scheduled for Wednesday evening, October 21, 2026, at the MERIT Center in Marshall, according to reporting from the Marshall Independent. The gathering at 1001 West Erie Road begins at 6:00 p.m.

The first panel features the race for Minnesota House District 15A at 6:00 p.m., followed immediately by the Minnesota Senate District 15 panel at 7:00 p.m. Participating candidates in the House race are Republican Brad Hennen and DFL candidate Nathan Schmig. The Senate discussion will feature Republican candidate Braxton Seifert and DFL candidate Andrea Boettger.

Both legislative seats feature an entirely new slate of contenders this election cycle. Long-serving incumbents Representative Chris Swedzinski and Senator Gary Dahms previously announced they would retire and step down at the conclusion of their current terms, the Marshall Independent reported.

The two districts cover wide sections of southwest Minnesota. House District 15A encompasses all of Lyon, Yellow Medicine, and Lac qui Parle counties, along with the city of Granite Falls in Chippewa County. Senate District 15 covers a larger territory comprising Lac qui Parle, Yellow Medicine, Lyon, Redwood, and Brown counties, as well as a portion of Blue Earth County.

Representatives from the Marshall Independent and Marshall Radio will serve as moderators for the panels. Participating candidates will answer questions prepared by a panel of local journalists as well as questions submitted directly by audience members. For residents tuning in from home, the entire program will be broadcast live on the radio by KMHL AM 1400 and televised locally by Studio 1 TV.

The forum provides area voters a final chance to compare candidate platforms ahead of the general election on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, according to the Marshall Independent.
