The City of Fargo and MATBUS are running Try MATBUS Week from Monday, October 26, through Saturday, October 31, 2026. All fixed-route bus rides across the Fargo-Moorhead metro network will be completely free for passengers throughout the six-day event.

As reported by WDAY Radio, the fare waiver applies strictly to standard fixed-route buses and does not cover MAT Paratransit services. The regional transit network currently logs nearly 2 million passenger rides each year. Organizers described the promotional week as an interactive effort to bring new commuters into that regular rider count.

Most of the daily giveaways will happen downtown at 222 4th St. N. That location took over transit hub operations after structural issues were discovered at the former Ground Transportation Center at 502 NP Ave.

On Tuesday, October 27, customer service staff at the downtown hub will hand out free reusable shopping tote bags while supplies last. On Wednesday, October 28, the transit center will host Warm-Up Wednesday, offering complimentary hot cider and donuts from a lobby self-serve station between 8:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m., with a limit of one per person.

Staff will distribute touchscreen-compatible winter gloves at the customer service desk on Thursday, October 29, while supplies last. On Friday, October 30, the lobby will feature free freshly popped popcorn and bottled water from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., limited to one bag and one drink per rider. On Saturday, October 31, drivers will hand out Halloween treats directly to passengers aboard the buses until supplies run out.

New riders can view instructional videos on MATBUS.com, plan trips using transit tools on Google Maps or Apple Maps, and track vehicles live on RideMATBUS.com. Customer assistance is available by calling 701-232-7500 (option 1) or via the agency's accounts on Facebook, X, and Instagram. Regular fares return once the promotion ends on Saturday, with MATBUS directing riders toward mobile contactless payments on the MATBUS Connect app.
