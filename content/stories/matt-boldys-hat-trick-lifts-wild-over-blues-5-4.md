The Minnesota Wild defeated the St. Louis Blues 5-4 on Thursday night at Xcel Energy Center in St. Paul, as reported by the Associated Press and KARE 11. The win improved Minnesota's season record to 5-2-1, kept the team undefeated at home, and marked their third victory in four games.

Winger Matt Boldy led the offense with four points, collecting his fifth career NHL hat trick and his first of the 2026-27 season along with an assist, according to the Associated Press and WDAY Radio. Boldy brought his season total to five goals and became the third-fastest active American skater to reach five career NHL hat tricks by doing so in 369 games, Ground News reported.

St. Louis opened the scoring at 1:45 of the first period when Jonatan Berggren scored on a power-play backhand, Yardbarker reported. Boldy answered for Minnesota at 7:31 of the period, tying the score 1-1 on a 5-on-3 power play with a one-timer from the high slot off a pass from Quinn Hughes. Danila Yurov gave the Wild a 2-1 lead at 11:17 of the first on a 3-on-2 rush fed by Maxim Shabanov, according to WDAY Radio and Big News Network.

Yardbarker reported that Mason McTavish tied the game 2-2 for the Blues on a breakaway five-hole goal at 4:57 of the second period. Boldy broke the tie at 5:24 with a wrist shot from the right circle off a feed from Joel Eriksson Ek. Kirill Kaprizov pushed the Wild's lead to 4-2 at 8:47 of the second period, deflecting a centering attempt from Hughes for his league-leading ninth goal of the season, according to the Associated Press. Boldy finished his hat trick at 16:47 of the second with a one-timer from the left circle set up by Kaprizov, making the score 5-2.

The Blues pushed back in the third period. Robert Thomas scored short-handed at 16:00, and Dylan Holloway scored with 15 seconds remaining to bring St. Louis within one, according to Yardbarker. Cam Fowler finished the night with three assists for the Blues.

Hughes finished with two assists, extending his season-opening point streak to eight consecutive games. The Associated Press and KARE 11 reported that the streak established a Wild franchise record for the longest season-opening scoring run. Hughes also became only the third defenseman in NHL history to open a regular season with an assist streak of at least eight games, according to the Associated Press.

Kaprizov added two assists to go with his goal, extending his scoring run to 11 points over his last five games, Field Level Media reported. Late in the third period, Kaprizov went to the dressing room after Blues goaltender Joel Hofer shoved him into the boards, drawing an interference penalty. Bring Me The Sports and Big News Network reported that team staff indicated Kaprizov appeared to be fine after the game.

Eriksson Ek finished with two assists, while Michael McCarron and Yakov Trenin each added an assist for Minnesota. Wild goaltender Jesper Wallstedt stopped 20 of 24 shots in the win, while Hofer made 33 saves as Minnesota outshot St. Louis 38 to 24.

Injured Wild defenseman Brock Faber, who has been sidelined with a preseason head injury, attended the game and received a massive ovation on the arena videoboard, Bring Me The Sports reported. Head coach John Hynes noted that Faber recently had stitches removed and has an upcoming doctor appointment to assess his return timeline.

Minnesota hosts the Columbus Blue Jackets at 5 p.m. Central time on Sunday, while the Blues travel to play the Nashville Predators on Saturday.
