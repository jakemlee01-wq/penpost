Signs posted at Crossroads Center show that Maui's Indoor Theme Park is preparing to move into the former Macy's department store space, according to reporting by WJON.

Crossroads Center officially updated its online retail and entertainment directory to list Maui's Indoor Theme Park as coming soon. The arrival of the complex will re-anchor a substantial section of the St. Cloud shopping center left vacant after retail closures.

The planned venue will feature indoor go-karts, bowling, laser tag, and virtual reality experiences. WJON reported that the entertainment center will also include bumper cars, arcade games, and a dedicated children's play area.

The project is owned and operated by Jester FEC, a family entertainment company based in Lexington, Kentucky. WJON confirmed the identity of the operator and the concept through the project contractor.

This St. Cloud site marks Jester FEC's first entertainment complex in Minnesota. The company already operates indoor theme parks under both the Maui's and Malibu Jack's names across five other states, including Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, and Colorado.

An official opening date has not yet been listed on site signs or announced for the venue. WJON reached out to Jester FEC for details on the construction schedule and timeline, but company representatives had not responded at the time of publication.
