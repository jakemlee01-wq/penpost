A red Chicago Bulls jersey worn on the court in 1998 now has a new private owner after a record-setting transaction. Pennsylvania-based Hunt Auctions announced on Monday, October 12, 2026, that an autographed, game-worn Michael Jordan jersey sold privately for $14 million.

The sale established an all-time price record for any piece of basketball memorabilia, according to Hunt Auctions. The firm identified the buyer as Ron Firman, a Miami real estate investor and executive originally from Rye, New York. Firman said in a news release reported by The Associated Press that Jordan was an incredibly talented player admired around the world, adding that he was very excited about the purchase.

The Associated Press reported that Jordan wore the red road jersey during Game 2 of the NBA Finals on June 5, 1998, against the Utah Jazz in Salt Lake City. Field Level Media reported that Jordan recorded 37 points, five rebounds, and three assists in the game, lifting the Bulls to a 93-88 victory that tied the series 1-1. Jordan and Chicago ultimately won the series in six games, securing his sixth NBA championship and his sixth Finals MVP award in his final season with the team.

Authentication firms MeiGray and SIA Photomatch photo-matched the jersey specifically to that Game 2 appearance, according to Hunt Auctions. The Associated Press reported that a private collector originally bought the jersey directly from the Chicago Bulls before this private transaction took place.

Hunt Auctions handled the sale as a private treaty transaction, which is a direct negotiated purchase between parties rather than an open bidding contest. David Hunt, president of Hunt Auctions, claimed that Jordan's standing in the memorabilia market has escalated to an elite asset class more typical for iconic works of art, adding that he was pleased to facilitate the sale.

The Associated Press reported that the $14 million price tag is the second-highest ever paid for any game-worn sports jersey. It trails only the $24.12 million paid in 2024 for Babe Ruth's New York Yankees jersey from his 1932 "called shot" World Series game.

This marks the third jersey from Jordan's 1998 Finals run to surpass $10 million, according to The Associated Press. His Game 1 jersey sold for $10.1 million through Sotheby's in 2022, and his Game 3 jersey sold for roughly $12.3 million through JOOPITER in September 2026.
