There's an easy way to spend a little money where it counts this weekend. Minneapolis's third annual Latino Business Week runs through Sunday, Oct. 11.

Saturday brings a pop-up market of Latino-owned businesses along Historic Main Street. On Sunday, Mercado Central on Lake Street hosts a celebration with shopping, hands-on activities and demonstrations at its restaurants. The weekday events were aimed at owners themselves, with networking and skills sessions.

The timing matters to the people running these shops. City leaders and owners told MPR News that a federal immigration enforcement surge slowed business this winter, and that many Latino-owned shops had already seen fewer customers before that. Part of this week's funding comes from relief money the mayor and city council approved earlier this year to help businesses recover income lost during Operation Metro Surge.

Rodrigo Cardoso, who runs a shipping and professional services business inside Mercado Central, said some businesses have recovered, while others have closed or are still struggling to keep up. Mayor Jacob Frey pointed shoppers to East Lake Street, Central Avenue and Nicollet Avenue.

MPR didn't report how many businesses closed or how much revenue was lost, so the size of the hit is still hard to pin down. The invitation itself is simple: show up, look around, and buy something.
