The Minneapolis Public Housing Authority announced the opening of its application waitlist on Tuesday, October 13, 2026, according to reporting from Affordable Housing Hub.

The waitlist applies to Minnesota's new state-funded Bring It Home rental voucher program.

To be eligible, applicants must maintain a primary residence in Minnesota. Qualifying households must also currently pay more than 30% of their income toward rent.

Under the program rules, applicants cannot be currently enrolled in or receiving subsidies from federal Section 8 Housing Choice Vouchers.
