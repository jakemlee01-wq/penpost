From boat builders and bait shops to ski hills and trail gear makers, getting outside is big business in Minnesota. State officials are now teaming up with partners across the country to give those companies more support.

On October 12, Minnesota became the 20th state to join the Confluence of States, according to Explore Minnesota. The multi-state coalition works to promote and protect the country's $1.1 trillion outdoor recreation economy, the group reported.

The economic footprint back home is already significant. Figures from the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis show that outdoor recreation generates about $13.5 billion in economic activity here. That makes up roughly 2.8 percent of the state's gross domestic product, which measures the total value of goods and services produced.

Across Minnesota, the sector supports approximately 96,000 jobs, according to Explore Minnesota. That includes workers in hospitality, manufacturing, conservation, and retail.

Randolph Briley, deputy director with the Minnesota Outdoor Recreation Industry Partnership, said joining the coalition opens a new chapter to strengthen business partnerships and support long-term economic stability. For the shops and outfitters spread across the state, having state agencies organized behind them may mean better resources to keep doing what they do best.
