Fans at Allianz Field in Saint Paul watched a two-goal deficit disappear on Saturday afternoon, Oct. 17, 2026, as Minnesota United FC rallied to defeat St. Louis CITY SC 3-2. The result gave Minnesota United its first home victory since April 18 and ended an eight-match winless streak, according to reporting from the Associated Press and MLSSoccer.com. The win also halted St. Louis City's 19-match MLS unbeaten streak, which CBS Minnesota and Major League Soccer noted had been the longest active run in the league.

St. Louis took an early lead four minutes into the match on an unassisted left-footed shot inside the box from Rafael Navarro, according to match reports from St. Louis CITY SC and the Associated Press. The Associated Press reported that the goal brought Navarro's combined season total to 18, keeping him fourth in the MLS Golden Boot race. Six minutes later, former Loons attacker Sangbin Jeong scored from the center of the penalty box off an assist from Navarro to put St. Louis ahead 2-0 heading into the halftime break.

Minnesota started its comeback four minutes into the second half. Midfielder Aziel Jackson scored his first goal of the season and his first as a Loon in the 49th minute, set up by Kelvin Yeboah and Bongokuhle Hlongwane. In the 68th minute, Yeboah tied the match 2-2 with a right-footed shot off a delivery from Mauricio González. The Associated Press and MLSSoccer.com reported that the strike was Yeboah's club-record 17th goal of the season and extended his streak to 10 consecutive matches with a goal contribution.

Marcus Caldeira provided the game-winner in the 86th minute with a left-footed shot from just outside the penalty box, assisted by Joaquín Pereyra and Hlongwane. Outlet Waking The Red and MLSSoccer.com reported that the goal was Caldeira's seventh in 12 league matches, coming 17 minutes after he replaced González in the 69th minute. Loons goalkeeper Drake Callender finished the afternoon with four saves, while St. Louis goalkeeper Roman Bürki stopped six shots, according to the Associated Press.

The loss ended a 21-match unbeaten streak across all competitions for St. Louis and stopped its 13-0-6 league run, denying the club a standalone MLS regular-season record and leaving it tied with the 2004 Columbus Crew. St. Louis defender Rafael Santos also marked his 100th career MLS appearance during the match. Following the match, head coach Cameron Knowles praised his squad's composure to battle back during the second half.

The Associated Press reported that the victory brought Minnesota United to an 8-12-10 record and 34 points, pulling the club within four points of ninth place and the final Western Conference playoff spot with four matches left. Minnesota travels to Houston Dynamo FC on Oct. 24 and hosts Sporting Kansas City on Oct. 28. St. Louis CITY SC faces the Columbus Crew in the U.S. Open Cup Final on Oct. 21 before traveling to play Real Salt Lake on Saturday.
