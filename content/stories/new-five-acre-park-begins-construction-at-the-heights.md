Ground is turning on a new five-acre park on Saint Paul's East Side. Construction officially began on Monday, October 19, 2026, marking the start of work on a central gathering spot inside The Heights, the 112-acre redevelopment of the former Hillcrest Golf Club near Larpenteur Avenue and McKnight Road.

Saint Paul Parks and Recreation expects work on the recreation space to finish in 2027. Once complete, the park will feature a children's play area, flexible lawn spaces, and a combined court designed for both basketball and futsal, which is a version of soccer played on a hard court. The city also plans a dedicated winter sledding hill, along with an open picnic shelter, a central restroom building, picnic tables, and outdoor barbecue grills.

MinneapoliMedia News reported that the layout includes natural plantings, preserved trees, and newly planted trees. MinneapoliMedia News also reported that the park will display public art structures described as tree creatures. An official permanent name for the park is being evaluated following public surveys and will be formally announced soon, according to Saint Paul Parks and Recreation.

Neighborhood residents helped guide the park design during community sessions. Engage Saint Paul noted that the planning effort included free youth art workshops held at the Hayden Heights Recreation Center in March 2025, where young people ages 10 to 18 worked alongside local artists to design original concepts. MinneapoliMedia News reported that design details were also shaped by input gathered at the Hayden Heights Library. City officials stated that Saint Paul project planners and design partner AFLA presented concepts and collected feedback at East Side events, including an outreach stop at a Bingo Night at Cups and Cheers. To prepare for ongoing upkeep, AFLA and project leads collaborated with Saint Paul Operations and Maintenance, Recreation Staff, and Natural Resources teams to refine operations and maintenance plans.

The park sits at the center of a broader redevelopment led by the Saint Paul Port Authority. A master plan approved in 2022 calls for approximately 1,000 housing units and 1 million square feet of light-industrial space, which provides room for light manufacturing, clean assembly, and neighborhood jobs.

Housing development across the property involves Sherman Associates, JO Companies, and Twin Cities Habitat for Humanity. Twin Cities Habitat for Humanity broke ground on up to 147 affordable homes across 12 acres of the site. According to the Saint Paul Port Authority, the central five-acre park is part of a larger network that will ultimately encompass 20 acres of green space, trails, and restored wetlands across The Heights.
