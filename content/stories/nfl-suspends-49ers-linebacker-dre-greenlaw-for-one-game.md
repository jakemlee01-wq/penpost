The NFL suspended San Francisco 49ers linebacker Dre Greenlaw for one game without pay on Monday, Oct. 12, 2026, for unsportsmanlike conduct, NBC Sports and the league reported.

The penalty followed an altercation during San Francisco’s 32-29 victory over the Seattle Seahawks on Sunday, Oct. 11, according to reporting from the Associated Press and WSLS. During the second quarter, Greenlaw drew a personal foul for hitting Seahawks quarterback Sam Darnold late out of bounds near the Seattle sideline, according to NFL.com.

Greenlaw then used his helmet to headbutt Seattle linebacker Ernest Jones IV, Pro Football Rumors and the league reported. Jones was not wearing a helmet at the time and was left bleeding, triggering Greenlaw's immediate ejection under Rule 12, Section 3, Article 1.

According to NFL.com and NBC Sports, Greenlaw is slated to miss San Francisco’s Oct. 19 game against the Washington Commanders before becoming eligible to return on Oct. 20, pending an appeal under the collective bargaining agreement.
