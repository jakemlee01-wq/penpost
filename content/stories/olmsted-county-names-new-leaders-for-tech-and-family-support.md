Olmsted County announced a leadership restructuring on Wednesday, October 14, 2026, shifting senior leadership across two major departments. The moves affect Information Technology Solutions, known as ITS, and Family Support and Assistance, known as FSA.

Meridee Braun, who has served as the director of FSA, was appointed as the county's new ITS Director. She steps into the post to succeed Matt Peabody, whose upcoming departure prompted the leadership transition.

In her new assignment, Braun will guide Olmsted County's long-term technology vision, oversee countywide technology planning, direct IT policies, and manage technological resource allocations to meet organizational goals.

Braun has worked for Olmsted County since 2002. Her tenure includes 19 years in Family Support and Assistance, where she spent the past four years as department director. She also spent five years, from 2017 to 2022, in ITS management, where she led project managers and business analysts through countywide technology rollouts.

James Johnson, Deputy Administrator of Administrative Services, stated that Braun brings extensive leadership experience, a strategic mindset, and a valuable perspective to the ITS director post, pointing to her past work guiding countywide technology projects. Johnson also thanked Peabody for his leadership and contributions to ITS over the past several years.

Dan Jensen was promoted to succeed Braun as director of Family Support and Assistance. Jensen has served as the associate director of FSA since 2022.

As director, Jensen will oversee policies and programs that supply basic needs for adults and families with children, aimed at promoting well-being, financial stability, and self-sufficiency.

Jensen brings more than 36 years of service with Olmsted County. Most of his career was spent with Olmsted County Public Health Services, including a period as associate director from 2011 to 2019.

Amy Rauchwarter, Deputy Administrator of Olmsted County Health, Housing, and Human Services, commended Jensen for his deep background, pointing to his steady, consistent leadership during a period of evolving work.
