The Otter Tail County Housing & Redevelopment Authority is seeking a licensed real estate broker to market and sell three single-family homes in Parkers Prairie. The county agency posted the request for proposals as an open, competitive procurement process on Oct. 14 and Oct. 15, 2026.

Real estate brokers must submit their proposals no later than 4:00 p.m. on Tuesday, Oct. 27, 2026. Proposals are being accepted electronically as PDF files sent by email to housing@ottertailcounty.gov.

The housing agency is based at the Otter Tail County Government Services Center, located at 500 West Fir Avenue in Fergus Falls. County housing officials Tanya Westra and Mitch Harry are fielding questions about the requirements by email, and inquiries can also be made by phone at 218-998-8730.

The three properties correspond to the agency's Oakwood Estates new construction initiative in Parkers Prairie. According to records from the Minnesota Housing Finance Agency, the project received state funding recommendations totaling more than $1.6 million, including financing allocations of $840,450 and $708,000, to expand workforce and affordable homeownership opportunities.

Otter Tail County noted that the sales are part of ongoing efforts to revitalize small-city housing stock and move residences into private, long-term ownership. The Otter Tail County Board of Commissioners appoints the housing authority's board, which was established in 1973 to address local shortages of affordable housing and rehabilitate substandard properties.

Recent housing market tracking from Edina Realty and Zillow shows that homes in Parkers Prairie have spent an average of roughly 45 days on the market, with median sales prices between $172,000 and $185,000.

Following the Oct. 27 deadline, the housing authority will review the submitted proposals to select and contract with a brokerage, which will then list the homes for sale to qualifying buyers.
