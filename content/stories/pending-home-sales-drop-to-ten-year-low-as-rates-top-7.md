Fewer buyers signed deals on houses this fall as 30-year fixed mortgage rates surged past 7 percent, according to Redfin.

Pending home sales across the country dropped 4 percent month-over-month on a seasonally adjusted basis in September 2026, marking the steepest monthly decline in more than three years. Redfin and Real Estate News reported that signed purchase contracts sank to their lowest level since spring 2014, setting aside the April 2020 pandemic shutdown.

Redfin found that contract signings fell across all 50 of the largest metropolitan markets in the country for the first time in three years. According to reporting from PR Newswire and Real Estate News, Seattle saw the steepest year-over-year drop at 24.4 percent, followed by Los Angeles at 16 percent, San Diego at 15.8 percent, and Anaheim at 15.5 percent.

Deals also came undone before closing. Redfin recorded that 14.5 percent of home-purchase agreements nationwide fell through in September, the highest share of contract cancellations in its data. At the same time, total housing inventory rose 1.2 percent from August, pushing active listings to their highest mark since March 2020, according to Redfin and the National Association of Realtors.
