At The Shoppes at Flemington, just off the Flemington Circle, morning diners can now tap a digital kiosk for takeout or sit down in a dining room built for 65 guests. The new Perkins Griddle & Go opened at 100 Reaville Avenue on Monday, October 19, 2026, marking the company's first fast-casual location in the United States, according to reporting from QSR Magazine and Franchising.com.

The restaurant is owned and operated by New Jersey residents Giuseppe and Nicole Lahara under their company Fork & Knife LLC. The Flemington address is the first of 16 Griddle & Go locations the Laharas have committed to opening across the state over the next 10 years. That commitment grew out of an earlier 10-unit development deal, which the couple later expanded by adding six units across Monmouth and Ocean counties. Giuseppe Lahara stated that as longtime fans of the brand, they wanted to bring a modern twist to something familiar while building a business their children can grow into.

The Flemington shop operates daily from 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m., combining digital menu boards and self-order kiosks for quick takeout with traditional café-style table seating. The kitchen serves familiar Perkins staples, including Mammoth Muffins, signature pies, and Griddle Greats, alongside newer fast-casual items like grab-and-go breakfast sandwiches, benedict bowls, and avocado toast.

Created in partnership with design firm The Aria Group, the format takes up between 1,500 and 2,000 square feet, as reported by Restaurant Dive. That compact layout makes the store roughly a quarter of the size of legacy Perkins restaurants, which typically span 6,000 to 6,500 square feet.

New Jersey has become the central launch market for this smaller format. Outlets including RestaurantNews.com reported that between 36 and 46 Griddle & Go units have already been sold across Mercer, Middlesex, Monmouth, and Ocean counties. Matt Carpenter, president of Perkins Restaurant & Bakery, stated that the company is taking what guests have loved for generations and bringing it to life in a completely new way.

Perkins was founded in 1958 and was recently rebranded from Perkins Restaurant & Bakery to Perkins American Food Co. by its Atlanta-based parent company, Ascent Hospitality Management, which also owns Huddle House. Ascent has also signed franchise agreements to expand the Griddle & Go model into Texas, California, and Canada, following an initial international pilot that launched in Canada in late 2024, according to Foodservice Equipment Reports Magazine.
