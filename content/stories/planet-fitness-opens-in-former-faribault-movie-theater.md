Planet Fitness has opened a new fitness center inside the former Cinema 6 theater building in Faribault. According to reporting from Southernminn.com and the Faribault Daily News, the gym marked its grand opening on Saturday, October 10.

The Faribault Area Chamber of Commerce hosted the ribbon-cutting ceremony and community tours at 10:00 a.m. on Saturday. Early visitors were greeted by staff members handing out complimentary swag bags and guiding tours across the workout floor. Local radio station KYMN (95.1 FM / 1080 AM) held a live broadcast on-site from 10:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m., with host Ned “The Horse Farmer” Newberg handing out prizes and sharing updates.

Completing the conversion of the theater building into a fitness center was a year in the making, according to Planet Fitness marketing specialist Maya Carson. Speaking to Southernminn.com, Carson said the city previously had a limited number of local gym options. She stated that the company chose Faribault to offer high value at a low price point, noting that the chain focuses on maintaining a clean, friendly, and functional environment across all of its clubs.

The redeveloped space features cardio and strength equipment, certified trainers, and group fitness classes. It also includes Black Card spa amenities, such as tanning, hydro massage, and infrared sauna options.

Southernminn.com reported that Black Card memberships are available for $24.99 per month, which include access to the spa amenities, guest privileges for every visit, and entry to all 2,900 Planet Fitness locations chainwide. For the opening, the business also ran a promotional rate through October 16 that offered enrollment for $1 down and $15 a month.

Carson told Southernminn.com that initial membership pre-sales were strong. She added that the club's staff is now focused on building regular foot traffic and drawing more local residents into the converted theater space.
