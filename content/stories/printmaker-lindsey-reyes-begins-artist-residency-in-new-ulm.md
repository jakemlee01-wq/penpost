Minnesota-raised printmaker Lindsey Reyes has moved into the basement studio of The Grand Center for Arts & Culture in downtown New Ulm for an artist residency running October 1 to October 23, 2026. According to reporting from The Journal and details from The Grand Center, she is working at Cellar Press, a letterpress and printmaking shop located at 210 North Minnesota Street.

During the stay, Reyes is producing a limited fine-art edition of 10 multi-color reduction block prints, with each print measuring 18 inches by 24 inches. In a reduction linocut, an artist creates an entire layered image from a single linoleum block, progressively carving away more material between each ink application so that the block is permanently reduced as each color is printed.

The specialized presses at Cellar Press make it possible to produce larger pieces that would be impractical to burnish by hand, Reyes told The Journal. She said that working with a single block that changes irreversibly forces her to bring an idea forward and let it go through a cathartic process, teaching herself to embrace mistakes while remaining connected through the meditative carving routine. Having a residency in a printmaking studio had long been a personal goal, she noted, adding that working in her home state holds special meaning for her.

The imagery in her new series draws inspiration from the Art Nouveau movement and Czech artist Alphonse Mucha, The Journal reported. Dense leaves, apples, and harvest foliage obscure a central human figure across the prints, examining themes of fantasy, isolation, and escape. Reyes explained that the botanical elements evoke an overflowing abundance and examine what she described as the magic inherent in our world. She noted that nature serves as a symbol of fantasy to capture the feeling of being lost inside an imagined space, adding that fiction at its best offers perspective shifts and reflects human experience.

Reyes discovered the opportunity through an open call listed on Springboard for the Arts. She graduated from Iowa State University in 2023 with a Bachelor of Fine Arts degree in Integrated Studio Arts, and she later taught elementary school art in North Carolina during the 2023 to 2024 academic year through the ArtistYear AmeriCorps program. Currently based in Jamestown, North Dakota, Reyes is serving a year-long artist residency teaching kindergarten through fifth-grade art across five elementary schools. She arranged a month-long break from that teaching position to complete her New Ulm project.

The Grand Center for Arts & Culture provides visiting artists in the Cellar Press program with 24-hour studio access, private lodging at The Grand Apartment, and a $300 weekly living stipend. While in town, Reyes also shared her craft directly with the community, hosting a class called Relief Printed Bookmarks at Cellar Press where local residents learned how to carve and pull small multi-layer prints.
