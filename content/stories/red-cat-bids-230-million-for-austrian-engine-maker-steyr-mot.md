Red Cat Holdings, a Salt Lake City defense robotics company, is offering approximately $230 million to buy Austrian engine manufacturer Steyr Motors AG. GlobeNewswire reported that Red Cat announced the voluntary public tender offer (an open bid to buy shares directly from stockholders) on Monday, Oct. 19, 2026, through its Vienna subsidiary SMOT Acquisition GmbH.

The cash offer stands at 39 euros per share, or about $43.80, which Reuters reported values Steyr Motors at roughly 204.8 million euros across its 5.25 million shares. According to Trending Topics and Investing.com, that price delivers a 28.9% premium (an amount offered above current market price) over Steyr's closing price on the Xetra exchange on Oct. 16. Investing.com and EQS News added that the bid is 27.3% above Steyr's 30-day volume-weighted average price and 55.8% above its 52-week low.

Trading reacted immediately to the cash offer, with Steyr Motors shares jumping over 20% in European trading and Red Cat rising 6.6% in U.S. pre-market action, as reported by Investing.com.

Like securing the engine before building the fleet, Red Cat is pursuing the deal to guarantee propulsion systems for its Blue Ops uncrewed surface vessel division, according to Trending Topics. That division relies on Steyr engine technology for robotic craft like the V7 and plans to manufacture more than 1,000 uncrewed vessels in 2027. Red Cat pledged to keep Steyr's headquarters, manufacturing plant, and research operations in Austria, GlobeNewswire reported.

Anchor shareholder B&C Group, which holds roughly 15% of Steyr's shares through B&C Holding Oesterreich GmbH, signed an irrevocable commitment to tender its entire stake, according to Reuters. Steyr Chief Executive Officer Julian Cassutti announced he intends to tender his personal shares, telling GlobeNewswire that the all-cash offer gives shareholders a chance to realize their investment in cash today. Steyr Supervisory Board Chairman Rolf Wirtz told Investing.com that leadership views the deal favorably, subject to reviewing the formal offer document.

The formal offer revives combination talks between the two companies that previously stalled in July 2026, according to Investing.com and Trending Topics. Evercore is serving as financial adviser and Freshfields is serving as legal counsel to Red Cat.

Closing the purchase requires crossing a minimum acceptance threshold of 50% plus one share and receiving foreign direct investment clearances across Austria, Italy, the United Kingdom, and Australia, according to Investing.com and Finimize. Investing.com reported the tender acceptance window remains open until Dec. 18, 2026, with Red Cat targeting completion in the first half of 2027.
