Redwood Falls will host the 2027 Minnesota Governor's Pheasant Hunting Opener, according to an announcement from the Office of Governor Walz. The 15th annual event is scheduled for October 8 and 9.

Governor Tim Walz stated that the area is an exceptional setting for the weekend, pointing to its parks, businesses, and natural resources. The Redwood Falls Chamber of Commerce applied on behalf of the community. State officials selected the host city through a process that weighed available hunting land, travel opportunities, and local support.

Anne Johnson, executive director of Redwood Area Chamber and Tourism, stated that hosting the event offers a chance to put a statewide spotlight on local organizations, businesses, and the Minnesota River Valley.

Redwood Falls sits along the Minnesota River Valley National Scenic Byway in Redwood County and shares history with the Lower Sioux Indian Community. The local economy relies on agriculture, research, manufacturing, healthcare, and tourism. The city is also known for Ramsey Park, a 256-acre municipal park often called the Little Yellowstone of Minnesota.

Initiated by Governor Mark Dayton in 2011, the event has visited 10 different communities. The Minnesota Department of Natural Resources and Explore Minnesota will assist local partners with planning the upcoming weekend.
