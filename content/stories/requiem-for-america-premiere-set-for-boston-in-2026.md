White Snake Projects announced details for the U.S. premiere of Mohican composer Brent Michael Davids' orchestral work, "Requiem for America: Singing for the Invisible People," OperaWire reported.

The concert is scheduled for November 14, 2026, at New England Conservatory's Jordan Hall in Boston, according to BroadwayWorld.

The project was co-commissioned with The Lenape Center to present an Indigenous perspective on the country's founding, the New England Conservatory reported.

BroadwayWorld noted that the composition blends orchestral music with Indigenous choral traditions. The piece pairs historical Native correspondence with colonial texts covering 17 states.

Conductor Tianhui Ng will lead the performance, OperaWire reported. The cast features vocalists Talise Trevigne, Ann Marie Stanley, Sara Couden, John Bellemer, and Leroy Davis.
