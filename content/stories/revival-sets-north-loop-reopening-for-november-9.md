A familiar name in Twin Cities fried chicken is moving into a new neighborhood. Revival will officially reopen its doors to the public on Monday, November 9, 2026, at 730 Washington Avenue North, according to the North Loop Neighborhood Association.

The launch marks Revival's first brick-and-mortar return to Minneapolis after abruptly closing all of its Twin Cities locations in January 2025. Following those shutdowns, restaurant group Jester Concepts acquired the Revival brand, proprietary recipes, logos, and digital properties, Bring Me The News reported.

Revival originally debuted in south Minneapolis in 2015 under founders and chefs Thomas Boemer and Nick Rancone, establishing a devoted following for Southern comfort food. Bring Me The News reported that Boemer and Rancone were brought back as consultants on the relaunch.

Brent Frederick, owner and founder of Jester Concepts, stated that he reached out to the founders immediately after hearing Revival was closing to see how his group could help. Frederick said they had always been fans of what Boemer and Rancone built, calling the brand a natural fit for Jester Concepts because of its special place in the Twin Cities dining community.

The new location takes over the storefront formerly occupied by Borough, Jester Concepts' first restaurant. The Heavy Table reported that Borough closed on June 27, 2026, concluding more than 13 years of operation in the North Loop. Directly below the dining room, basement cocktail lounge Parlour Minneapolis underwent brief renovations from June 28 to July 6, 2026, and remains open for business.

Revival announced that the flagship will serve weekday lunch, dinner, and weekend brunch. Operating hours are Monday through Thursday from 11 a.m. to 9 p.m., Friday from 11 a.m. to 10 p.m., Saturday from 10 a.m. to 10 p.m., and Sunday from 10 a.m. to 9 p.m.

The kitchen will feature Revival's signature menu, including Southern Fried and Tennessee Hot fried chicken, classic sides, and the Revival Burger.

Advance reservations opened to the public immediately through OpenTable. Ahead of the November launch, management invited patrons holding unredeemed gift cards from the prior ownership to submit their details through an online intake form.
