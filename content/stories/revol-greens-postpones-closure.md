Revol Greens has pushed back the planned closing of its greenhouse near Owatonna, a change that could save dozens of jobs if a pending deal comes through.

The company grows lettuce and other greens year-round in a 10-acre, climate-controlled greenhouse that opened in 2017. In August it told state regulators it would close the facility on Oct. 4 and lay off nearly 100 people. A revised notice filed in early October puts that on hold. Interim CFO Michaele Gobel wrote that the postponement is "due to ongoing efforts to close a transaction that may allow operations at the facility to continue," MPR News reported.

If the deal falls through, the company expects the closing and layoffs to happen between Oct. 15 and Oct. 29. MPR's report also cites 89 employees who would be laid off, a figure it didn't reconcile with the earlier estimate.

The company has said a national Cyclospora outbreak linked to iceberg lettuce cut demand across the leafy greens business and complicated its financing talks, though none of its own products were contaminated.

For the people who work there, the next three weeks matter most. A postponement isn't a rescue yet, but it keeps open a door that looked closed in August.
