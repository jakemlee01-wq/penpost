Patients calling into clinics in Aitkin, Garrison, and McGregor can reach a representative around the clock, book expanded appointments, and work with dedicated care coordinators.

Those services are part of why the Minnesota Department of Health announced on Monday, Oct. 12, 2026, that Riverwood Healthcare Center recertified those three primary care clinics as Level 3 Minnesota Health Care Homes. Level 3 is the highest tier in the state program, building on foundational and Level 2 clinical quality standards.

To reach that top tier, clinics must show strong integration between clinical care delivery and community public health work. Riverwood noted that this includes participating in population health improvement, contributing to local community health needs assessments, and tracking public health initiatives.

According to the Minnesota Department of Health, the Health Care Home system aims to improve patient health outcomes and lower overall medical spending. The model helps patients proactively manage chronic conditions before they require emergency treatment. Riverwood stated that the coordinated approach is designed to ease care transitions, reduce unnecessary emergency room visits, cut hospital readmissions, and prevent adverse health events.

Inside the three clinics, Riverwood employs seven registered nurse care coordinators who work alongside the primary care teams. These coordinators handle chronic disease management, principal care management, and behavioral health integration for approximately 324 patients with complex medical needs.

Dr. Tim Arnold said the nurse care coordinators monitor hospital patient lists and incoming messages, handle emerging problems, and follow up regularly with patients. Arnold noted that this work helps reduce chronic disease complications and new medical issues while allowing him to provide better care.

Heidi Olesen said the Level 3 certification demonstrates that the clinics offer high-quality care, address social determinants of health, and focus on community health while seeking ongoing ways to improve patient outcomes.

Led by CEO Ken Westman, Riverwood Healthcare Center serves patients throughout Aitkin County and the surrounding Mille Lacs Lake region. In addition to the three primary care clinics, the organization operates a 25-bed critical access hospital in Aitkin and employs more than 525 caregivers across the area.
