Three hundred jobs is a lot of new name badges. That's how many Meridian Bio Devices says it will add at its Rochester plant over the next three years. (Demo story; the company and figures are examples.)

The company makes small parts used in heart monitors and infusion pumps. It plans to add a second production building on land it already owns and says the project will cost about $85 million. A state job creation grant listing shows a $1.5 million award tied to the hiring, paid only if the jobs actually show up.

That last detail is worth noticing. Grants like this are usually conditional, so the public money follows the hiring rather than the press release.

Most of the new jobs are production and technician roles, according to the company, with training offered through a nearby technical college. For people in southeast Minnesota, that's a door into steady work that does not require a four-year degree.

It also matters beyond Rochester. Minnesota has a big cluster of medical device companies, and suppliers like Meridian are part of what keeps that cluster here.

Plenty can change in three years, and the company has not said how quickly hiring will start. But it's a hopeful plan, and one worth following.
