Shawn Owen Dieser, 41, was sentenced in Olmsted County District Court after pleading guilty to felony intentional discharge of a firearm endangering safety, according to reporting from KROC-AM News. A gross misdemeanor charge of negligent firearm storage was dismissed as part of an agreement finalized shortly before trial.

The conviction stems from an incident on the night of August 22, 2025, at the Zumbro Ridge Estates manufactured home community in the 700 block of 55th Street Northeast, according to KROC-AM News and the Olmsted County Sheriff's Office.

Deputies arrived after a woman walked out of the residence to alert officers. Inside the mobile home, deputies discovered Dieser heavily intoxicated and asleep on a couch. Two adolescent boys were also sleeping inside the home, located across the hall from a bedroom where deputies recovered a loaded shotgun.
