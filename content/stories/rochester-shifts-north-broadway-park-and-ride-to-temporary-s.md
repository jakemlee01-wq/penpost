Rochester Public Transit announced on Tuesday, Oct. 13, 2026, that the North Broadway Park & Ride lot will temporarily relocate starting Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026.

Commuters using the current temporary lot at the former Chateau Theater CMX parking site, at 3450 East Circle Drive Northeast, will shift to the City of Rochester Transportation Center parking lot at 4300 East River Road Northeast.

Rochester Public Transit confirmed that Routes 21, 120, and 150 will continue serving the Park & Ride from the East River Road facility. Timetables and run times for all three routes will remain on their regular schedules without adjustments.

The interim setup is scheduled to stay in place until early 2027, when a new permanent transit facility is slated to open for operations.

Crews are already building that permanent hub on the northeast corner of the Rochester Recreation Center property off Elton Hills Drive Northwest. The upcoming project will feature a 200-space concrete parking structure that adds a net 140 spaces to the grounds, along with a sheltered passenger waiting area, restrooms and facilities for drivers, and a transit stop built to accommodate two buses at the same time.

Once finished, the permanent site will operate as a primary transit hub where several routes converge, allowing riders to transfer between lines.

Federal funding is covering the construction, backed by a U.S. Department of Transportation grant alongside a Federal Transit Administration Bus and Bus Facilities grant.

City transit officials emphasized that express park-and-ride routes provide direct trips to Saint Marys Hospital and downtown Rochester while helping reduce road congestion. Rochester Public Transit reminded riders that express trips to both destinations are also running from two other permanent lots: the 75th Street Park & Ride at 7340 Prairie Vista Drive Northwest, and the Fairgrounds Park & Ride at 14th Street Southeast and Frontage Road Southeast.
