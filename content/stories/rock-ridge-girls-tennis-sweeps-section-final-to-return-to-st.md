The Rock Ridge Wolverines girls tennis team secured its second consecutive trip to the state tournament with a 7-0 dual match sweep over the North Branch Vikings. The Section 7AA final was played on Tuesday, Oct. 13, 2026, at the Virginia Indoor Tennis and Pickleball Club in Virginia.

The matchup was a rematch of the previous year's Section 7AA championship, where Rock Ridge also defeated North Branch. As reported by the Mesabi Tribune, the Wolverines won every singles and doubles contest in straight sets without dropping a single set across the entire match. The sweep keeps Rock Ridge undefeated in Section 7AA team competition for the second straight season.

A dual match is a team competition decided by total individual match wins. Rock Ridge secured the team victory early, building an insurmountable 5-0 lead while the final two individual contests were still underway.

Rock Ridge entered the postseason as the defending Class AA state champion, having won the program's first Class AA title the previous autumn by defeating Blake. With the section crown, the Wolverines advance to the Minnesota State High School League Class AA state team tournament bracket at Life Time Bloomington South in the Twin Cities.

In singles play, senior Mayme Scott defeated North Branch senior Rebekah Wurdemann 6-3, 6-1 at No. 1 singles. At No. 2 singles, freshman Ellie Dimberio beat junior Evelyn O'Donnell 6-2, 6-1. Sophomore Alice Johnson took the No. 3 singles match against senior Eliana Smit 6-1, 6-0.

The doubles teams completed the sweep. The Mesabi Tribune reported that seniors Nina Paulsen and Emmi Peterson defeated North Branch seniors Charlotte Santjer and Julia Schlagel 6-2, 6-3 at No. 1 doubles. At second doubles, Kiri Milos and Erin Troutwine defeated Ainslee Hunter and Sophia Podoll 6-3, 6-1. At third doubles, Avery Scott and Arie Bialke won their match against Brianna Bjerketvedt and June Peterson 6-0, 6-2.

Following the win, Rock Ridge coach Tom Prosen praised the roster's fortitude, calling the team the hardest working group the coaches have had. Coach Jim Prittinen acknowledged that North Branch is a quality team that made the Wolverines work for the win. Senior Emmi Peterson remarked that while players sometimes just want to finish the match, their opponents were great players who made things tough at the end.

Individual competition followed quickly. As reported by the Mesabi Tribune and Grand Rapids High School Athletics, team members immediately turned around to compete in the Section 7AA individual singles and doubles tournament at Cloquet High School.
