Supporters of the Andover-based nonprofit Superman Sam's Survival Kit will gather on Friday, October 23, 2026, for the fourth annual "In Your Shoes" Night Out. The fundraiser is scheduled from 6:00 p.m. to 10:00 p.m. at the Bunker Hills Event Center, located at 12800 Bunker Prairie Road NW in Coon Rapids.

According to S3 Kit's event listing, more than 250 people are slated to attend the snappy casual evening. The gathering includes dinner, dessert, a guest program, raffles, a photo booth, and both live and silent auctions. For supporters unable to attend in person, the organization opened digital bidding through the Givebutter platform.

S3 Kit announced several donated auction items, including a seven-day Holland America cruise, a Minnesota Wild sports package, and a garage floor makeover donated by Swedebro. Bidders can also participate in auctions for hotel stays, golf outings, dining certificates, and family entertainment experiences.

FOX 9 and S3 Kit reported that proceeds from ticket sales, sponsorships, and the auction will directly fund care packages for pediatric tracheostomy patients. A tracheostomy is a surgically created opening in the neck into the windpipe to help a child breathe. S3 Kit's GiveMN profile notes that a $150 donation covers an entire basket along with nationwide shipping costs, while $50 funds clothing supplies and $20 pays for two hanging medical organizers.

Executive Director Jamie Grant founded the 501(c)(3) nonprofit in 2021, drawing from her experience raising her fourth child, Sam. Doctors discovered a hole in Sam's airway at two months of age, requiring a tracheostomy tube. According to the nonprofit's website, Sam endured 80 surgeries and procedures, three cardiac arrests, and a 40-day medically induced coma before receiving a lifesaving breathing tube.

The leadership team later expanded to include Assistant Director Lynn Fields, whose son Calvin received a tracheostomy in 2018 following lung failure. According to organization records, Fields joined the nonprofit's board in 2021 before moving into the assistant director role in 2024 to help guide community outreach and fundraising.

The organization ships complimentary care baskets to any caregiver or hospital requesting one across the country. S3 Kit disclosures show the group has distributed more than 225 customized baskets nationwide since 2020, with 60 delivered in 2024 alone.

Each package is tailored to a child's medical diagnosis. The baskets hold practical items such as size-specific side-snap onesies, superhero shirts, bandana bibs to protect stomas, and mesh washing bags for gastrostomy tube pads. They also include stroller and car-seat germ warning signs, hanging organizers for home medical supplies, a custom tracheostomy plush doll, and the illustrated children's book "I Breathe Through My Trach." Wendy, a supporter quoted by the nonprofit, stated that the basket lets families know they are not on this journey alone and gives them the confidence they can do this.

FOX 9 reporter Leah Beno spotlighted the organization and the Coon Rapids fundraiser on Sunday night, October 18, 2026, detailing how the care packages ease the transition from hospital to home for pediatric patients.
