Shake Shack announced it will officially open its fifth corporate-run Minnesota restaurant on Wednesday, Oct. 21, 2026, in Minnetonka.

The fast-casual restaurant, a style of dining that pairs counter ordering with made-to-order food, is located at 1809 Plymouth Road, Suite 1, directly across from Ridgedale Center. Bring Me The News reported that the building was constructed on the redeveloped parcel of a former Wells Fargo bank branch, a project led by OneCorp Global and Wells Fargo that had been in development since 2024. The site sits next to a Chipotle Mexican Grill, which opened at 1807 Plymouth Road at the end of July 2026.

According to the Excelsior-Lake Minnetonka Chamber of Commerce, opening day activities will begin before public service starts at 10 a.m. The chamber is scheduled to co-host a ribbon-cutting ceremony between 9:30 a.m. and 10 a.m., with the ribbon cut planned for 9:40 to 9:45 a.m. The first 100 customers through the doors will receive a custom tote bag and a $5 voucher toward a future visit.

Shake Shack also announced it will donate $1 for every sandwich sold during opening day to the Intercongregation Communities Association Food Shelf, a local food shelf based in Minnetonka.

City records reported by Bring Me The News show that the Minnetonka location applied for on-sale wine and 3.2 percent malt beverages to accompany its core lineup of smash burgers, crinkle-cut fries, chicken sandwiches, and hand-spun milkshakes.

Operating hours at the Minnetonka restaurant will run from 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. Sunday through Thursday, and from 10 a.m. to 11 p.m. on Friday and Saturday.

The Minnetonka opening adds to Shake Shack's existing retail footprint in the Twin Cities, which includes locations at Southdale Center in Edina, Mall of America in Bloomington, Rosedale Center in Roseville, and The Shoppes at Arbor Lakes in Maple Grove, along with a licensed counter at Minneapolis-St. Paul International Airport. Bring Me The News reported that additional Twin Cities sites are currently in development near Eden Prairie Center and The Shoppes at Woodbury Lakes.
