Six rescued research beagles arrived at an Eden Prairie home on October 10 after a 1,200-mile van trip from an animal testing laboratory in Houston, Texas, according to reporting from Eden Prairie Local News and The Beagle Alliance.

Two Eden Prairie residents who work as volunteer coordinators for The Beagle Alliance organized the operation, with the dogs arriving at the home of coordinator Jill Schumacher. 

All six beagles are between 3 and 5 years old. According to the rescue group and Eden Prairie Local News, the dogs had spent their entire lives indoors inside laboratory kennels prior to the transport. 

The Beagle Alliance gave the six animals celestial names for their arrival: Nova, Sirius, Major, Gemini, Orion, and Draco. New owners met them upon arrival, including St. Louis Park residents Stephanie and Justin Goetz, who attended to adopt one of the dogs.

The Beagle Alliance reports that more than 60,000 dogs are held in laboratory testing environments across the United States each year, with most bred specifically for research facilities.
