An old cannery site in Sleepy Eye is moving toward a new occupant. The Sleepy Eye City Council held the first reading of an ordinance to sell the former Del Monte Corp. property to Heartland Properties for $600,000 in cash, The Journal reported.

Heartland Properties of Andover, LLC, which also operates as Heartland Tire, intends to convert the property into a commercial warehouse. City Manager Bob Elston announced the warehouse plan, noting that the buyer is targeting a closing date before the end of 2026. The transaction covers all parcels enclosed within the perimeter fence at the industrial complex.

Councilor Nate Stevermer introduced the first reading of the sale ordinance. Alongside the sale, the council unanimously approved a resolution to set a November 10, 2026, public hearing regarding a 15-year property tax abatement of up to $400,000 and a business subsidy package. Councilor Kristina Andres moved the resolution, which Stevermer seconded.

A tax abatement reduces or refunds property taxes to offset specific costs. In this case, the abatement allows the city to recoup municipal funds used to help facilitate the $600,000 price tag. Elston stated that the site is expected to generate approximately $30,000 in annual city taxes, which will reimburse the city until the $400,000 is fully paid off. Elston added that the agreement includes a requirement to create one full-time job paying about $25 to $30 an hour, with final details to be settled at the November 10 meeting.

The 215,000-square-foot site has changed hands several times since Del Monte closed its vegetable cannery in August 2019. That plant operated for nearly 80 years as the town's largest employer, and its closure laid off more than 360 workers. Seneca Foods bought the facility in 2020 and later sold it to a pet food producer. An investment firm acquired the property after that, and the site eventually fell into foreclosure.

Sleepy Eye Public Utilities stepped in to buy the property at a foreclosure auction for $600,000 in March 2026. The utility purchased the land to safeguard local utility interests and return the vacant facility to commercial use. The city council will take up the public hearing and consider final approval of the sale on November 10, 2026.
