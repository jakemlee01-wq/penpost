Walk past the north side of Macy’s at Southdale Center, and you will see 13-foot brass-framed glass doors leading into a newly built dining room. Inside, the 225-seat space is finished with rift-cut white oak paneling, bronze lion sculptures, a Carrara marble bar, and a Harry Potter-inspired booth labeled "9¾."

This is Smithson Steak & Seafood, scheduled to open its doors on Saturday, Oct. 17, 2026. The new luxury concept at 10 Southdale Center, Suite 2160, is owned and operated by Edina-based restaurateurs Randy and Sharon Stanley. Bring Me The News reported that this marks the third establishment in their suburban steakhouse portfolio, following 6Smith along Lake Minnetonka in Wayzata and Baldamar at Rosedale Center in Roseville.

Randy Stanley described Smithson as their most upscale endeavor yet. According to Bring Me The News, he stated that the team wanted to create a legacy restaurant that feels special enough for a celebration, but comfortable enough to visit every week. General Manager Adam Rosenkrantz told the Minnesota Star Tribune via Hoodline that the restaurant's scale gradually unfolds as patrons approach, adding that it was built with the structural and culinary ambition to last for 100 years.

To manage the front of the house, the Stanleys brought in Rosenkrantz, who carries more than 15 years of industry experience, including opening the Graze food hall in Minneapolis and managing Borough and Parlour Bar. Operations for the Stanleys' broader restaurant group are overseen by Chief Operating Officer Michael Symons, who was promoted after serving as the longtime general manager at Baldamar, the Minneapolis / St. Paul Business Journal reported.

The kitchen is led by Executive Chef Chris Dmochowski. The Minneapolis / St. Paul Business Journal and Hoodline reported that Dmochowski brings over 25 years of experience to the role, having spent nearly eight years as executive chef at CoV in Wayzata, nine years at Campiello in Eden Prairie, and opening Boardwalk Kitchen & Bar in Shakopee.

Dmochowski's menu centers on hand-cut Midwestern beef aged for 40 days and 40 nights without added hormones or antibiotics, finished under an 1,800-degree broiler. The Stanleys claim a Twin Cities first by serving Creekstone Farms Irodori Wagyu ribeye. Reporting from Bring Me The News and Wisevoter shows dinner entrées starting around $38, with certified prime cuts priced between $65 and $90, and starters and sides ranging from $17 to $28.

Beyond steak, the menu features swordfish en brodo with Taggiasca olives, Salmon en Croûte, king salmon steak with chermoula, birria short ribs, ricotta gnocchi with corn purée, and cider-braised Beeler's bacon with bourbon cherries. The restaurant also offers caviar service. The bar program highlights boutique and heritage spirits, including Old Forester 100 and Michter's rye.

Smithson plans to operate Monday through Thursday from 11 a.m. to 9 p.m., Friday from 11 a.m. to 10 p.m., Saturday starting at 10 a.m., and Sunday from 10 a.m. to 9 p.m.

The arrival aligns with Simon Property Group's $400 million phased redevelopment of Southdale Center, which is reshaping the property into an upscale retail, lifestyle, and dining destination.
