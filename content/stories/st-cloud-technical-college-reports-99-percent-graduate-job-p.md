Graduates leaving the campus on Northway Drive in St. Cloud are stepping straight into the local workforce. St. Cloud Technical & Community College announced that 99 percent of its 2025 graduates who were seeking work in their field found jobs, as reported by WJON News. That figure marks the highest employment rate among all colleges and universities in the Minnesota State system.

The college awards roughly 1,009 associate degrees, diplomas, and certificates each academic year. Several of its technical career programs recorded a 100 percent job placement rate in students' fields of study. More than 24 percent of the student body consists of first-generation college attendees, meaning they are the first in their families to pursue higher education.

Under Minnesota State system guidelines, member institutions survey former students within one year of graduation. The surveys track whether graduates found work in their field, took jobs outside their area of study, or continued their education. To validate the employment numbers, each participating school must achieve at least an 85 percent response rate from its graduates.

Lisa Mohr, the career services coordinator at the college, stated that the numbers show strong student preparation and the school's ability to respond to what employers need. Mohr credited regional collaboration for the outcome, pointing to support across academic programs, students, employer partners, faculty, staff, and the broader community.

The college's placement numbers have hovered in the upper 90s for several years. That includes a 98 percent placement rate recorded for the class of 2024. To help students find work sooner, the career services office replaced its traditional job fair with ongoing Career Connections events held throughout the school year. According to the college, those regional partnerships frequently lead local St. Cloud employers to extend job offers to students while they are still taking classes.

The school's reach extends across the state. College figures estimate that 19,680 alumni currently live and work in Minnesota. College calculations estimate that those graduates generate roughly $2.9 billion in lifetime economic impact while working statewide.

Campus enrollment is also climbing. Fall 2026 enrollment grew by 38 students compared to fall 2025, which marks an overall 17 percent increase in enrollment since fall 2021.
