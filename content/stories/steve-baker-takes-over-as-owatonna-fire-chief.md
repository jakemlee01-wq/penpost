Retirement from municipal service lasted less than a year for Steve Baker. The City of Owatonna announced his hiring as the new fire chief on Monday, Oct. 12, 2026, as reported by ABC 6 News and a city news release. Baker stepped into the position the same day, bringing more than 25 years of emergency services, training, and leadership experience.

Baker had stepped down from active municipal fire service in November 2025 to launch Ignite Leadership Solutions, LLC. The firm provides leadership development and training programs for fire service agencies. While his business grew, Baker noted that he missed direct work in the fire service.

That pull brought him to Steele County. Baker said he researched Owatonna, toured the facility, and met with Assistant Fire Chief Jake Dashiell before applying. In a city statement, Baker said the chance to serve a community that takes pride in its culture and public services stood out to him.

City Administrator Jenna Tuma stated that Baker's depth of experience, energy, and collaborative approach will serve the organization and the community well. She noted that he will lead an already dedicated team at the Fire Hall.

Baker built his emergency services career across several Minnesota agencies. He previously served as assistant fire chief in Plymouth and in Golden Valley. His resume also includes working as a paramedic for North Memorial Ambulance and serving as a tactical paramedic with West Metro SWAT.

Describing his leadership philosophy, Baker said his role for many years was to help people on the worst day of their lives. He explained that his new focus in Owatonna is to help the helpers by supporting the firefighters and ensuring they have what they need to serve the public.

The new job also comes with a change of address. Baker and his wife, Julie, are scheduled to relocate their residence from Maple Grove to Owatonna on Nov. 1, 2026.
