City of Mankato officials reported that officers responded around 12:30 a.m. on Sunday, October 11, 2026, to a report of two people fighting in the 500 block of South Front Street.

When officers arrived, they saw that one of the individuals was carrying a handgun. The armed suspect ran from the scene on foot, prompting officers to pursue him.

Police caught up to the suspect, leading to a physical struggle on the ground as officers worked to take him into custody. During that struggle, the suspect's handgun discharged. Officials reported that no one was injured by the gunfire.

The suspect, identified as a 29-year-old man from St. Paul, was taken into custody and transported to a local hospital as a precaution.
