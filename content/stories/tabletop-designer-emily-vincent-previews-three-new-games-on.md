Emily Vincent is putting three tabletop games in front of players this month, ranging from prairie botany to late-night household takeover by cats.

St. Cloud radio station WJON reported that the tabletop game designer and publisher appeared on its bi-weekly program Table Talk during the 8:00 a.m. hour on Saturday, October 17, 2026. The show is hosted by WJON news reporter Paul Habstritt alongside retail gaming expert William Pankratz from Games By James. This was Vincent's second appearance on the broadcast, following an earlier segment about her pastry-and-piracy title, Pirates of the High Teas.

Vincent used the appearance to preview Perennial, a strategy tile-placement game currently funding on Kickstarter. The project is Vincent's second design collaboration with Flatout Games, following their previous release, Knitting Circle. The title features illustrations by Beth Sobel.

Perennial belongs to the polyomino genre, a category of puzzle games that use multi-square geometric figures. Tabletopia and GeekDad noted that the game plays in 30 to 45 minutes, with players drafting square tiles that match tool cards and arranging them into polyomino shapes. The goal is to cultivate a blooming North American prairie garden. Scoring rules shift each game, rewarding players for building habitats that attract native wildlife such as owls, chipmunks, and rabbits. Vincent said the design spent multiple years in development and originated as an entirely different gameplay concept before evolving into a botanical theme.

Vincent also announced that her independent label, Pink Hawk Games, is launching a separate dual-title Kickstarter campaign on Tuesday, October 20, 2026. Both titles in that campaign focus on cats.

The first, Good Kitties, is a cooperative strategy game where players control cats attempting to take over a home overnight while their human owners sleep. During the radio interview, Habstritt and Vincent nicknamed the game's custom cat-shaped meeples, or wooden player pieces, Meeowples.

The second title, Super Chonk, is a card game designed by Ben Jones. As reported by WJON and Cardboard Court, the game plays in about 15 minutes as participants race to shovel kibble into hungry cats' mouths to make them chonky. Super Chonk marks the first title published by Pink Hawk Games that Vincent did not design herself.

WJON published its feature on Sunday, October 18, 2026. The station reported that Table Talk will return on Saturday, October 25, 2026, when Pankratz and Habstritt plan to review board games for the Halloween season.
