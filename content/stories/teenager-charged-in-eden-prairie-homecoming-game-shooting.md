Adam Abdihakeem Adam, an 18-year-old resident of St. Louis Park, has been charged in Hennepin County District Court following a shooting outside Eden Prairie High School, according to reporting by CBS Minnesota and FOX 9. He faces two counts of second-degree assault with a dangerous weapon and one count of second-degree riot with a dangerous weapon.

The charges stem from an Oct. 2 shooting outside Aerie Stadium during Eden Prairie High School's homecoming game against Minnetonka, CBS Minnesota and EP Local News reported. The gunfire wounded two teenagers in the leg and foot, bringing the game to a halt.

FOX 9 and CBS Minnesota reported that police surveillance footage showed approximately 10 people fighting near parked vehicles. A driver then stepped out of a Hyundai Sonata and opened fire. Investigators subsequently matched Adam's mobile phone data to the car's movements.

Adam posted a $100,000 bond and was released from the Hennepin County Jail on Saturday evening, Oct. 10, according to FOX 9 and CBS Minnesota. Both outlets reported that his initial court appearance in Hennepin County was scheduled for the afternoon of Monday, Oct. 12.
