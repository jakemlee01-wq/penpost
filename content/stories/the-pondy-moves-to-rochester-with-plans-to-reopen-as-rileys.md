Steve and Devra Overton are relocating their destination eatery 15 miles south into Rochester, according to Bring Me The News. The Pondy Restaurant & Bar officially ended its eight-year run on Lake Zumbro in Mazeppa when it closed on Sunday, Oct. 4, KROC-AM reported.

The restaurant is rebranding as Riley's Restaurant and targeting an opening date of Dec. 1, 2026, according to KROC-AM.

Bring Me The News reported that the owners cited the compounding impact of inflation on food prices and seasonal drops in customer traffic as primary reasons for leaving their Mazeppa lakeside setting. In a farewell message to patrons, the Overtons called the move emotional and bittersweet, noting that it marked their first Friday in eight years without sourcing inventory and prepping specials.

Riley's Restaurant will take over the building at 1643 1/2 N. Broadway Ave., Bring Me The News reported. That space was previously home to Glynner's Pub, which closed in 2025 after more than two decades in business.

The Overtons confirmed that they intend to keep their core menu intact, continuing to serve the comfort-food favorites that drew visitors to Lake Zumbro, KROC-AM reported.

According to KROC-AM, the move provides Rochester's north side with an experienced independent operator following other recent closures in the area, including Mill Valley Kitchen and Thesis Beer Project.
