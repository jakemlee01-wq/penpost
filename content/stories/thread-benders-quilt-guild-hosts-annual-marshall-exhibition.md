The Thread Benders Quilt Guild hosted its annual quilt exhibition at the Marshall Adult Community Center over the weekend of Saturday, Oct. 10, and Sunday, Oct. 11, 2026. The Marshall Independent reported that the event featured dozens of quilts and handmade fabric crafts created by regional quilters. Lila Schmidt reported that attendance surpassed 105 visitors by Saturday afternoon.

Cindy Bader, a Marshall resident, national quilt educator, and pattern designer behind Patterns on the Prairie, was the featured guest. Bader hosted trunk shows spanning more than 30 years of her work, the Marshall Independent reported.

Her interest in the craft started after graduating from college while working at a bank, where a quilt was assembled for a retiring employee. At the exhibition, volunteers helped Bader show the first quilt she ever made approximately 32 years ago. That initial project used cardboard templates, featuring Bible verses and sayings traced onto fabric with a light box. Bader said the piece was off by about half an inch in some spots, explaining that she still displays it to show how quilters improve with practice.

Bader also demonstrated her mosaic applique method, a technique that relies on Hotfix fusible adhesives and light-box tracing rather than traditional cardboard templates. The process leaves intentional narrow gaps between colored fabric pieces before fusing them to a background, producing a finish that resembles tile work.

The guild featured a raffle prize quilt based on the "Starlight" pattern by Wilmington Prints. Guild members Michelle Full and Phyllis Scholtes discovered the pattern at the Minnesota Quilt Show. A guild committee made up of Full, Scholtes, Marissa Kunde, and LeAnn Buysse pieced and sewed the quilt together. Scholtes then spent 80 hours completing freehand, custom longarm quilting stitches to fill the open background spaces. Before the exhibition, the finished quilt was displayed across the parking lot at the Graceful Marilyn Quilt Shoppe in downtown Marshall, managed by Heather Rokeh.

According to the City of Marshall, the Thread Benders Quilt Guild holds its regular meetings on the first Monday of each month at 6:30 p.m. at the Marshall Adult Community Center. Patterns on the Prairie noted that Bader is scheduled to teach a binding class at the Graceful Marilyn Quilt Shoppe on Oct. 18, as well as a paper quilt design session at the Young Arts Conference in Marshall on Oct. 29.
