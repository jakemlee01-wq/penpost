A tennis lineup that drops only six individual matches across three singles spots will turn some heads. Senior Sam Konz, junior Lydia Sweep, and freshman Lauren Sweep from the Morris Area/Chokio-Alberta/West Central Area girls tennis team earned spots on the West Central Conference All-Conference team on Tuesday, Oct. 13, 2026. The awards were reported by Stevens County Times sports writer Sam Peterson.

The trio anchored the state-ranked MACA/WCA Tigers throughout a dominant autumn. Junior Lydia Sweep played No. 1 singles for the entire season, compiling an 18-3 individual match record. Senior Samantha Konz rotated between the No. 2 and No. 3 singles positions, logging a 19-2 mark. Freshman Lauren Sweep also split time between No. 2 and No. 3 singles, finishing her regular season run at 19-1.

Those individual marks helped push the Tigers to an 18-2 overall regular-season dual record. The team secured the No. 10 ranking in Minnesota Class A and capped the regular schedule with a 4-3 road win against Benson/KMS. In that narrow victory, Lydia Sweep won 6-3, 6-2 at No. 1 singles, Lauren Sweep rallied back for a 4-6, 7-5, 6-1 win at No. 2 singles, and Konz took a 6-4, 6-2 victory at No. 3 singles.

Postseason recognition followed a rain-shortened appearance at the West Central Conference tournament on Oct. 3 in Alexandria. Weather ended play after the Tigers finished just four matches, but all three honorees won their opening bouts. Lydia Sweep entered as the top seed at No. 1 singles and swept Camryn Olesen of Lac qui Parle Valley/Dawson-Boyd 6-0, 6-0. Lauren Sweep held the second seed at No. 2 singles, beating Kendall Beuning of Melrose 7-5, 6-4. Konz was the second seed at No. 3 singles and defeated Sarah Seehusen of MACCRAY 6-4, 6-4.

The regular season results earned MACA/WCA the No. 1 overall seed in the Section 3A team playoffs. Postseason play began at home in Morris against eighth-seeded Luverne on Oct. 12. The winner of that contest was scheduled to meet either Yellow Medicine East or Lac qui Parle Valley/Dawson-Boyd in Sioux Falls on Thursday, Oct. 15.
