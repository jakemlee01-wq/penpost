The Minnesota Timberwolves built a comfortable road lead and defeated the New York Knicks 122-104 at Madison Square Garden, according to reporting from MinneapoliMedia.

Anthony Edwards and Jaden McDaniels led the offense for Minnesota, combining for 48 points in the blowout victory, MinneapoliMedia reported. SNY and WDAY Radio reported that Edwards led all scorers with 26 points, shooting 8-of-16 from the floor and 5-of-8 from 3-point range, while adding three rebounds and three assists in just under 25 minutes. McDaniels scored 22 points on 8-of-12 shooting, including 4-of-5 from deep, and gathered five rebounds. Fubo News noted that Edwards has averaged 19.7 points per game through early preseason play, while McDaniels has averaged 15.7 points, 3.7 rebounds, and one assist per game across the exhibition schedule.

The exhibition matchup offered another chance to integrate playmaker LaMelo Ball into the starting backcourt alongside Edwards, as reported by NBC Sports. Ball finished with 10 points and seven assists across 22 minutes, while bench reserve Ayo Dosunmu chipped in 11 points, according to an Associated Press box score published by Ground News.

New York took its only lead of the night early in the first quarter on a basket by Josh Hart, who finished with two points, three rebounds, and three assists, SNY reported. From there, Minnesota took control. Best of Sports Moments reported that the Timberwolves held a 12-point advantage at halftime and extended the margin to as many as 20 points during the second half.

Minnesota also controlled the glass, outrebounding the Knicks 39 to 28 despite no individual Timberwolves player pulling down more than five rebounds, according to SNY. New York struggled from the perimeter throughout the evening, shooting 45.5 percent from the field and 25 percent from 3-point range on 8-of-32 shooting, according to SNY and Empire Sports Media.

Jalen Brunson paced the Knicks with 20 points on 6-of-13 shooting, though SNY reported that Brunson and the rest of the New York starters were pulled after reaching a 22-minute limit. Former Timberwolves center Karl-Anthony Towns and wing Mikal Bridges combined for 20 points on 6-of-12 shooting for New York. OG Anunoby added 15 points, making all six of his free throws. SNY and the outlet Biggest Storylines Coming Out of Knicks' Preseason Loss reported that Anunoby was fouled hard on a transition dunk by Dosunmu, an exchange that ended with both players and the referees laughing it off.

After the game, Knicks head coach Mike Brown said his team's defense "needs to be better," according to SNY. Brown also praised backup center Andre Drummond's defensive presence, stating Drummond's communication is unbelievable right now.

The victory lifted Minnesota's exhibition record to 3-1, following earlier wins over Milwaukee and Miami and a single loss to Indiana, Fubo News reported. In the previous game, the coaching staff rested the projected starting lineup and Dosunmu, which allowed 26-year-old swingman Terrence Shannon Jr. to start and finish as the team's third-leading scorer, according to FOX Sports.

The loss dropped the defending champion Knicks to 0-3 in exhibition play before they traveled to Toronto for a back-to-back game with bench players, according to MinneapoliMedia Sports and Biggest Storylines Coming Out of Knicks' Preseason Loss. Minnesota now prepares for the 2026-27 regular season and returns home to Minneapolis to face the Detroit Pistons at Target Center on Wednesday, October 14, at 7:00 p.m. Central, Fubo News reported.
