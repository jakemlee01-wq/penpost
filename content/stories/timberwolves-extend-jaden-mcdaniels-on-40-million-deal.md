The Minnesota Timberwolves agreed to terms on a one-year, $40 million contract extension with forward Jaden McDaniels, according to reporting first broken by Shams Charania for ESPN and detailed by NBC Sports.

The agreement was reached Monday night and confirmed to ESPN by McDaniels' agents, Nima Namakian of Innovate Sports and Bill Duffy of WME Basketball. The extension attaches to McDaniels' existing deal for the 2029 to 30 season, raising his guaranteed pay over the next four seasons to $124 million.

McDaniels had three seasons and $84 million remaining on a five-year rookie contract extension originally signed in October 2023 for approximately $136 million, according to NBC Sports and BasketNews. HoopsHype and BasketNews reported that McDaniels is the first player in NBA history with three guaranteed seasons left on a non-maximum contract to sign a veteran extension.

Because McDaniels had multiple guaranteed years remaining, Monday served as the NBA league deadline to complete an extension for the 2026 to 27 league year, according to Hoops Rumors. Hoops Rumors also reported that his maximum starting salary could have reached roughly $41.74 million, giving Minnesota a modest discount below the allowable limit.

The mechanics of the deal also set up future flexibility. Under NBA rules, veteran extensions can start at up to 140% of the previous season's salary. ESPN and Hoops Rumors reported that lifting McDaniels' 2029 to 30 base salary to $40 million positions him to become eligible in October 2028 for an extension starting at 140% of that higher figure, projected at up to a three-year, $181 million agreement, rather than working off his previous $29.8 million cap figure.

According to an assessment reported by ESPN, McDaniels secured an additional $40 million guaranteed while maintaining the flexibility to negotiate another extension in 2028 or enter free agency in 2030 with 10 years of NBA service. Hoops Rumors and Bring Me The Sports noted that reaching a decade of league service by 2030 qualifies the 26-year-old forward for the maximum 35% salary tier in free agency.

The extension secures McDaniels alongside guard Anthony Edwards and newly acquired starting point guard LaMelo Ball, who are both under contract through 2029, according to NBC Sports and Hoops Rumors.

During the 2025 to 26 regular season, the 6-foot-9 forward averaged 14.8 points, 4.2 rebounds, 2.7 assists, 1.1 steals, and 1.0 block per game while shooting 51.1% from the floor and 41% from three-point range, according to Prime Time Sports. In the 2026 playoffs, McDaniels increased his scoring average to 16.3 points per game, NBC Sports reported. In the recent preseason, he averaged 24.8 points in under 21 minutes per contest while shooting 60.6% from the field and 71.4% from beyond the arc.

Over six seasons in Minnesota since being drafted 28th overall in 2020, McDaniels has averaged 11.1 points, 4.2 rebounds, 2.7 assists, and 1.1 steals per game, according to Prime Time Sports. The Timberwolves open the 2026 to 27 regular season on Wednesday, Oct. 21, on the road against the Miami Heat at 6:30 p.m. Central on ESPN.
