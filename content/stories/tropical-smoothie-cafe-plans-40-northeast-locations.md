Tropical Smoothie Cafe operates more than 1,750 locations across 44 states, but it entered 2026 with only 68 spots split between New York and Connecticut. On Monday, October 19, 2026, the Atlanta-based company announced a 40-unit franchise development agreement to change that balance, according to PR Newswire.

The push targets Albany, Westchester County, and the Bronx in New York, alongside Western Connecticut, Restaurant Dive reported. The partner behind the buildout is Aurify Kingstar, a newly established holding company created by New York multi-concept operator Aurify Brands, according to Nation's Restaurant News.

Aurify Kingstar is led by partners Andy Stern, John Rigos, and Jeremy Tannenbaum, who bring more than 25 years of restaurant industry experience, PR Newswire reported. The group has developed, operated, or acquired roughly 300 restaurants, including franchise locations for Five Guys, Wingstop, and Le Pain Quotidien, as well as proprietary brands like Melt Shop, Little Beet, and Happy Monkey by Jean-Georges.

Stern told Nation's Restaurant News that the group created Aurify Kingstar as a separate vehicle for franchise expansion "to keep it clean" while evaluating deals in other categories. Daily Voice and PR Newswire reported that Aurify Kingstar plans to open its first Tropical Smoothie Cafe in early 2027, though exact addresses and opening dates remain unannounced.

Tropical Smoothie Cafe Chief Executive Officer Max Wetzel stated via PR Newswire that New York and Connecticut represent substantial growth potential for the brand. Wetzel added that Aurify Kingstar brings the infrastructure and multi-unit development track record the chain looks for in expansion partners.

Tannenbaum told the Daily Voice that his team has tracked Tropical Smoothie Cafe's growth and saw an opening to build its footprint across the Northeast. The brand started the year with 62 stores in New York, representing 3.7% of its total footprint, with four openings planned for 2026, Restaurant Dive reported. Connecticut entered the year with six stores and two scheduled openings.

The franchised chain began 2026 with 1,651 locations and remains on track to open its 1,800th unit before the end of the year, according to Nation's Restaurant News and Restaurant Dive. Private equity firm Blackstone bought the company for approximately $2 billion in 2024.

The Northeast deal follows the company's recent appointment of Joe Sieve as chief development and growth officer to manage corporate expansion initiatives, PR Newswire reported. On September 16, 2026, the chain rolled out a rebranding campaign with a new logo centering on its "Eat Some Good!" philosophy, and both Nation's Restaurant News and Restaurant Dive reported the brand is pursuing international sites as well as non-traditional spots such as military bases and airports.
