An extended freeze-free stretch across the Twin Cities continues to prolong ragweed and pollen exposure for local allergy sufferers, MPR News reported.

As of Monday, Oct. 26, 2026, temperatures at Minneapolis to St. Paul International Airport have yet to dip to 32 degrees or colder. The typical first-freeze date for the metro area is Oct. 11, placing this autumn more than two weeks behind schedule.

Minnesota Department of Natural Resources records show that roughly 90 percent of autumn seasons in the Twin Cities record a temperature of 32 degrees or colder by Oct. 28. Historical weather records show that a first autumn freeze has held off until November only eight times: 1894, 1900, 1921, 1924, 1931, 1940, 1958, and 2016.

The latest first freeze ever recorded in Twin Cities history occurred on Nov. 18, 2016, according to MPR News and the DNR. That date arrived 11 days later than the previous historical record of Nov. 7, which was set in 1900.

Across the wider region, Midwestern Regional Climate Center data reported by MPR News shows that most of northern Minnesota has already logged its first freeze of the 2026 autumn season. Exceptions in the north remain limited to parts of Duluth and the Lake Superior North Shore. Outside the Twin Cities metro, areas south of St. Cloud have stayed above the freezing mark.

Northern Minnesota typically records its first freezing temperatures in September, while southern portions of the state generally see their first freeze in October, according to MPR News.

Freezing overnight lows do not appear imminent for the metro. MPR News Updraft reported that daytime high temperatures in the Twin Cities are forecast to rebound into the 60s from Tuesday through Friday, with highs in the 50s and 60s returning for the mid-to-late week. Overnight minimum temperatures are projected to stay well above freezing in the 40s through the end of the month.

With those mild overnight readings expected through Halloween, MPR News reported that the Twin Cities is on track to finish October without recording a freeze, pushing its first autumn freeze into November.
