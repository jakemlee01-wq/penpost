A calendar of set menus acts like a tasting tour across the metro: Mpls.St.Paul Magazine launched its 2026 Fall Restaurant Week on Monday, Oct. 19, running through Sunday, Oct. 25. The weeklong dining event is presented in partnership with Twin Cities home service company Standard Heating & Air Conditioning. More than 60 restaurants across Minneapolis, St. Paul, and surrounding metro suburbs are participating, according to the magazine.

The promotional dining roster features selections from Mpls.St.Paul Magazine's 2026 Best Restaurants list and culinary Hall of Fame alongside neighborhood staples. Organizers told diners to make plans for date nights or visits to local neighborhood gems during the promotion. Participating kitchens designed prix-fixe menus, meaning fixed-price multi-course meals, that span two to four courses and range from $15 or $25 up to $60 per diner.

In downtown Minneapolis, Tavola is serving a three-course lunch for $25 and a three-course dinner for $40. Nearby, Farmers Kitchen + Bar created a $26 lunch featuring Black Angus sliders, alongside a $50 dinner highlighting Wild Acres roasted chicken and chocolate flan. Downtown eatery Chloe, located at 700 3rd St. S., has also opened multi-course menus and table bookings for the week.

Over at the Graze North Loop food hall in Minneapolis, guests can order a $30 three-course meal that mixes appetizers, entrees, and desserts from different hall vendors, according to reporting from Mato Events. In south Minneapolis, Italian Eatery rolled out a four-course dinner for $55 across all seven nights, which includes a choice of appetizer, salad, house-made pasta, and dessert.

Across the river in St. Paul, Grand Avenue landmark supper club The Lexington opened multi-course menus and reservations. Mirabelle is offering a $45 three-course dinner menu highlighting comfort dishes, including chicken wild rice soup, lasagna, pan-seared branzino, and chocolate cake. In addition, Tria is serving a curated three-course fall dinner starting at $45, with optional wine pairings available alongside its regular menu.

Waterfront destinations west of the cities are also taking part. Birch's on the Lake Brewhouse & Supperclub in Long Lake is featuring a $30 two-course lunch and a $45 three-course dinner overlooking the water. In Wayzata, 6Smith is running three-course lunch and dinner menus priced between $30 and $60 at the Boatworks Building on Lake Minnetonka, with options like roasted harvest salad, wagyu meatballs, miso seabass, and filet mignon, according to 6Smith and City Pulse MN. On Lake Waconia, Lola's Lakehouse launched curated prix-fixe menus spotlighting prime steaks and seafood.

The promotional week wraps up on Sunday, Oct. 25, though select venues like 6Smith are extending their prix-fixe service through Oct. 26. After that, participating kitchens, including Tria, will transition to customer appreciation specials running from Oct. 26 to Nov. 1 ahead of Thanksgiving seasonal menus.
