More than 60 local directors, actors, and administrators gathered at Artistry in Bloomington to discuss operational changes needed to keep area venues solvent.

As reported by Twin Cities Business, the meeting followed an Oct. 12, 2026, industry report detailing severe post-pandemic financial headwinds straining regional nonprofit theaters. Deliberations were spurred by financial difficulties facing the Jungle Theater, which was founded on Lyndale Avenue in Minneapolis in 1991.

Theater leaders noted that a combination of reduced federal arts support and dwindling corporate donations has outpaced audience recovery. State appropriations for the arts also dropped 22% over the previous year, though Minnesota still remains among the national leaders in per-capita arts funding.
