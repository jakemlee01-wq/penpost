Two separate missing-person searches for endangered individuals in Douglas County were successfully resolved within 36 hours.

On Sunday morning, Oct. 11, 2026, 22-year-old Gregory Shoultz of Alexandria was located safely following an overnight alert, according to the Minnesota Bureau of Criminal Apprehension, the Alexandria Police Department, Bring Me The News, and WJON. Shoultz had gone missing on Friday, Oct. 9, with his last known location tracked near the Minnesota-Iowa border heading south toward Des Moines, Iowa.

The Alexandria Police Department had issued an urgent alert noting, "There are concerns for Gregory's safety and well-being." Authorities described Shoultz as 5 feet 7 inches tall and 150 pounds, with sandy blonde hair and hazel eyes, traveling in a slate-blue 2022 Subaru Legacy with Minnesota license plate ZDC878, as reported by WJON and police. The Minnesota Bureau of Criminal Apprehension shared the alert statewide on behalf of Alexandria Police and the Douglas County Sheriff's Office before canceling the alert on Oct. 11 after confirming he was safe.

The following afternoon, 57-year-old Debra Margaret White was located safe and unharmed on Monday, Oct. 12, 2026, at 4:53 p.m., according to the Douglas County Sheriff's Office and Valley News Live. The Douglas County Sheriff's Office posted an update stating, "UPDATE!! 10/12/26 at 4:53 PM Debra White has been located safe. Thank you for your assistance!"

White had last been seen on Saturday, Oct. 10, in the Monticello area before disappearing, according to KARE 11 and WJON. The Douglas County Sheriff's Office classified White as missing and endangered because she did not have required medical prescriptions with her.

During the search, investigators asked the public to watch for a blue 2012 Mazda 3 hatchback bearing Minnesota license plate PDP-218, KARE 11 and WJON reported. Authorities described White as 5 feet 7 inches tall, weighing approximately 135 pounds, with gray and blonde hair and multicolored eyes.

In both cases, Douglas County authorities routed public tips through dispatch at 320-762-8151 before both individuals were found and safely reunited, according to KARE 11, WJON, and the Douglas County Sheriff's Office.
