The University of Minnesota Alumni Association announced its 2026 Twin Cities campus Homecoming Grand Marshals on Monday, October 12, 2026. The association selected three alumni to share the honor: Pat Duncanson, Dr. Ken Holmen, and Carissa Carroll. The trio will serve as guests of honor across campus during homecoming events scheduled from October 22 through October 24, 2026.

The 2026 festivities coincide with the university's 175th anniversary milestone year. According to the alumni association, selecting three marshals reflects the institution's historical impact across agriculture, healthcare systems, and civic engagement. Lisa Lewis, the alumni association's president and chief executive officer, stated that the three alumni demonstrate how a degree creates practical impact and embodies the university's land-grant mission across the state and country.

Duncanson earned his undergraduate degree in 1983 as a first-generation student in the College of Food, Agricultural and Natural Resource Sciences. He serves as the chief executive officer and chief financial officer of Highland Family Farms in Mapleton, Minnesota. Duncanson also sits on the Regent Candidate Advisory Council, chaired the alumni association's board of directors from 2023 to 2024, and assists alumni governance on the Past Chairs Advisory Group. The association noted he also received the 2026 National Agricultural Alumni and Development Association Volunteer Service Award.

Dr. Holmen completed his medical degree at the university in 1978 along with his residency, and he serves as the president and chief executive officer of CentraCare Health. The university recognized Holmen for his work expanding rural healthcare and regional medical systems. That effort includes partnering directly with the Medical School to establish the CentraCare Regional Campus in St. Cloud, which trains physicians to meet medical staffing needs in Greater Minnesota.

Carroll completed her Master of Education through the College of Education and Human Development in 2008. She is the founder and chief executive officer of Jack's Basket, a nonprofit organization that assists families of newborns with Down syndrome. Carroll draws on her educational training to instruct medical providers internationally on delivering diagnoses using bias-free, compassionate language. She was named to Inc. magazine's 2026 Female Founders 500 list and received the National Down Syndrome Society's Betsy Goodwin Award.

The schedule for the three marshals begins Thursday, October 22, when they will receive formal honors and give remarks at the Alumni Awards Affair at Huntington Bank Stadium. On Friday, October 23, the group will lead the annual Homecoming Parade along University Avenue.

Events conclude on Saturday, October 24. The marshals will join the Ski-U-Mania pregame gathering at the McNamara Alumni Center and take part in on-field recognition during the homecoming football game at Huntington Bank Stadium.
