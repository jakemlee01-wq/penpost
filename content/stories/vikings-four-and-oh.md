Some teams win with fireworks. The Vikings are winning with a kicker and a very stubborn defense.

Minnesota beat the Miami Dolphins 15-10 on Sunday at U.S. Bank Stadium to start the season 4-0. Every one of the Vikings' points came from Will Reichard, who kicked five field goals. The offense didn't score a touchdown.

Quarterback Kyler Murray went 27 of 37 for 247 yards and was sacked four times, according to FOX 9. Afterward he put it simply: the team has "gotta put the ball in the end zone more."

The defense did the heavy lifting again. It held Miami to 198 total yards, forced a turnover, and sacked quarterback Malik Willis twice. That's a familiar pattern: two weeks earlier the Vikings beat Chicago 9-3, also on Reichard field goals.

A 4-0 record counts the same however you get there, and close wins are still wins. The open question is how long a team can keep winning low-scoring games before it needs touchdowns. For now, the defense and Reichard keep answering it.
