The Minnesota Vikings defeated the New Orleans Saints 23-13 on Sunday, October 11, 2026, pushing their record to 5-0 heading into their bye week, FOX Sports reported.

Running back Aaron Jones led the offense with 28 total touches in the road victory, according to CBS Sports. Quarterback Kyler Murray finished the day with 24 completions on 30 pass attempts for 229 yards and a touchdown, The Viking Age reported.

Wide receiver Justin Jefferson caught a 21-yard touchdown pass during his return to Louisiana, CBS Sports noted.

Kicker Will Reichard maintained a perfect field goal record for the season, according to Vikings.com. The team site also reported that linebacker Jake Golday forced a second-half fumble punchout to swing momentum.
