Meritage Hospitality Group operates 314 restaurants and is currently reorganizing under Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection, Nation's Restaurant News reported. The franchisee plans to shut down 41 locations across five operating groups after those selected stores produced $4.2 million in negative EBITDA, an accounting measure of basic operating profit.

Wendy's corporate filed formal objections to Meritage's plan, specifically contesting the closure of 10 restaurants located across Oklahoma, Michigan, Georgia, and Florida. Wendy's proposed substituting seven different locations instead, which corporate representatives claimed produce lower sales volume but smaller financial losses. Meritage rejected that corporate substitution proposal and is fighting attempts to terminate its franchise agreements.
