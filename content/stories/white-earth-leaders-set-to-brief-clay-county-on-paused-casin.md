White Earth Nation leaders are scheduled to appear Tuesday before the Clay County Board of Commissioners, Valley News Live reported. They plan to give a 20-minute informational briefing regarding their proposed Moorhead casino and hotel project.

The planned $176 million development has been on pause since August 2026 following the election of tribal Secretary-Treasurer Jacob McArthur, according to reporting from AM 1100 The Flag.

AM 1100 The Flag reported that the scheduled presenters are White Earth Nation Chairman Michael Fairbanks, Tribal Utilities Commission Executive Director Nate Mathew, and Land Acquisition Manager Jacob Syverson.

Their presentation outlines the federal fee-to-trust process as well as the two-part secretarial determination required by the U.S. Department of the Interior. County meeting materials state the agenda item is strictly informational and requires no formal action from the commissioners.
