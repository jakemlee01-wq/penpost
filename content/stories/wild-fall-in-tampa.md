The Wild lost 3-2 to the Tampa Bay Lightning on Thursday night, and most of the damage happened before the game was six minutes old.

Anthony Cirelli scored at 5:26 of the first period, and John Carlson added another 36 seconds later. Quinn Hughes got Minnesota on the board early in the second. Carlson then scored again on a power play to make it 3-1, and Blake Coleman's power-play goal, set up by Kirill Kaprizov, pulled the Wild within one. That was as close as they got.

Both goalies were busy, according to NHL.com. Dennis Hildeby made 34 saves in his first game for Tampa Bay, and Jesper Wallstedt made 32 for the Wild.

The Wild are 2-1-1 and have lost two straight. Coach John Hynes summed up the fix plainly: "We've got to play a full 60 (minutes)." Coleman put it another way: "You can't put yourself in a hole against a good team."

It's early, and four games is a small sample. But giving up two goals in 36 seconds is the kind of habit worth fixing before it becomes one.
