Winona State women's volleyball worked overtime Saturday, Oct. 17, 2026, pulling off a five-set win over the University of Mary at the McDowell Activity Center in Bismarck, North Dakota.

The Warriors took the contest 26-24, 25-22, 14-25, 24-26, and 19-17. University of Mary Athletics reported that Mary served for match point three separate times in the fifth set before Winona State reeled off the final three points to secure the 19-17 frame.

Winona State Athletics noted that the Warriors built an early edge by turning aside late pushes from Mary to claim the first two sets 26-24 and 25-22. The Marauders responded in the third set, hitting .393 with 13 kills and two errors on their way to a 25-14 win. Winona State built a 19-15 lead in the fourth set, but Mary closed with an 8-2 run capped by a Holli Klettheimer kill to grab a 26-24 win and force set five.

Zoe Haan and Abby Ongna paced the Winona State attack with 14 kills apiece, while Maddie Brown supplied 13 kills. University of Mary Athletics credited the Warriors with 63 kills, 68 assists, 78 digs, and eight blocks on the match.

For Mary, Dani Highum posted a career-high 25 kills with a .242 hitting percentage, pairing that with 11 digs, six blocks, three aces, and two assists. Judith Burke supplied 17 kills on a .317 hitting clip with seven digs for the Marauders, and setter Natalie Boehmer collected 42 assists, 14 digs, five kills, and two aces. Ruby Rengo recorded a career-high nine blocks and eight kills for Mary, which totaled 66 kills, a .217 hitting percentage, 13 team blocks, and 77 digs.

The victory finished a weekend sweep in North Dakota, following a 3-0 win against Minot State (25-12, 25-19, 25-21) on Friday night, Oct. 16, at the MSU Dome, as reported by the Winona Journal and team releases. The two wins lifted Winona State to 5-14 overall and 4-9 in Northern Sun Intercollegiate Conference play, while Mary dropped to 4-17 overall and 2-11 in the league.

Winona State next plays at McCown Gymnasium, hosting the University of Jamestown on Friday, Oct. 23, at 6 p.m., followed by Northern State University on Saturday, Oct. 24, at 2 p.m.
