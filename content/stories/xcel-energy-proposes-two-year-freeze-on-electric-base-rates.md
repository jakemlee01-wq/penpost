Xcel Energy announced on October 12, 2026, that it filed a proposal with the Minnesota Public Utilities Commission to keep electric base rates flat for two years. If state regulators approve the filing, the utility said it will delay its next rate case until at least November 2028.

During that two-year stretch, Xcel plans to invest approximately $4.5 billion into Minnesota's electric grid while keeping those base rates stable. The proposed system work includes $1.4 billion directed toward electric distribution, $1.1 billion for clean energy generation and battery storage, and $1 billion for transmission lines and infrastructure upgrades.

Bria Shea said the proposal also designates $600 million for technology, fleet, and property services. Another $500 million in the plan is budgeted to maintain operations at Minnesota nuclear power plants.
