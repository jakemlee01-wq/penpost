Zamboni’s on 7th is expanding by opening up an interior brick wall to reclaim the original storefront it occupied for 13 years.

On Wednesday afternoon, Oct. 14, 2026, the St. Paul City Council approved the expansion proposal submitted by co-owner Gregory Awada, according to reporting by the Minneapolis / St. Paul Business Journal. Awada originally filed the paperwork with the city in August 2026 to enlarge the sports bar and pizza restaurant across the street from Grand Casino Arena.

The approval clears the way for Zamboni’s, located at 174 7th St. W., to take over the vacant 1,000-square-foot storefront next door at 184 7th St. W. Awada plans to launch a new bar and restaurant concept called Zim’s in the space, operating under the same ownership. Tying the two spaces together will bring the establishment’s total footprint to approximately 6,000 square feet, situated between the current bar and Ticket King.

The project marks a return to familiar territory. The 1,000-square-foot site at 184 7th St. W. previously served as the original home of Zamboni’s Pizza Pub, where the business operated for 13 years before moving next door. In late 2023, Awada and co-owner Kristi Brunner teamed up with Tim Mahoney, owner of The Loon Café, to relocate Zamboni’s into the larger 174 7th St. W. building after Eagle Street Grille vacated following a 20-year run.

Under the renovation plans, workers will cut a passageway into Zim’s through the back right corner of Zamboni’s, opening up the interior brick wall that currently separates the two storefronts. Inside Zim’s, Awada plans to build a 40-foot bar and install an additional restroom.

To fund the project, Awada plans to secure a loan under a new limited liability company, or LLC, a corporate structure that protects personal assets. Once interior construction is complete, that new LLC will contract Zamboni’s original LLC to oversee food and liquor service at Zim’s.

Awada could not immediately be reached for comment after the council’s vote, the Minneapolis / St. Paul Business Journal reported.
